# Origin Trailer Principles — decoded from UMB's hit

**Purpose:** The BURNED origin trailer must match the thing that made
*Undercover Mob Boss*'s V3 origin trailer land. This doc decodes *why*
UMB hit and converts it into the acceptance bar for the BURNED origin
rebuild. It is the spec the new beat sheet has to satisfy.

**Source artifact:** UMB V3 narration script —
`../../../undercover-mob-boss/scripts/narrator-prompts.ts`
(`TRAILER_V3_PROMPTS`). The BURNED draft this replaces:
`../../videos/trailer/BEAT-SHEET.md`.

---

## The principle (reuse this)

> A true story about a **human making a bet**, where the autonomy is the
> **dramatized, escalating, honestly-owned payoff** of that bet — told in
> **concrete images**, with the **human cost** as the heart.

UMB proved an autonomy/build showcase CAN be the origin story and still
hit. It ends on *"One hundred percent autonomous SDLC... That... is the
product."* The showcase was never the problem. **How** the showcase is
told is everything.

---

## Five load-bearing elements

Each is something UMB got right and the BURNED draft dropped.

### 1. A human protagonist with a bet and a flaw
UMB's story is about a person: *"Briggsy didn't write a single line of
code. Not one."* → *"He picked a stack he'd never touched."* → *"He made
a bet that a machine could build something worth playing."* The machine
is the engine; the human is the character.
**BURNED draft failure:** no human, no bet, no one to follow. The
narrator (Dash) reads a roster. Nobody to root for.

### 2. Truth, told in concrete images — never laundered into jargon
Same content (test counts), opposite outcomes:
- **UMB (hit):** *"Thirteen hundred and thirty-one tests. Agents playing
  drunk, doing things no sane player would. Rage-tapping. Disconnecting
  mid-vote... Nothing broke."*
- **BURNED draft (flat):** *"Mission rehearsal: fourteen hundred and
  seven contingencies war-gamed. Six of them, deliberately unrehearsed."*

Near-identical real numbers (1331 vs 1407). UMB gives vivid, true, funny
images you can see. BURNED gives a spreadsheet in a trenchcoat.

### 3. Escalation to an earned punchline
UMB builds spec → swarm → code → the drunk-agent test gauntlet → deadpan
release: *"Nothing broke."* Tension, then snap. The BURNED draft's stat
cascade is a flat list paying off into *"They WERE the operation"* — a
punchline with no build under it.

### 4. Honest about being a flex, so it doesn't read as one
*"That is the product."* Owning the pride out loud is disarming — a
confession, not a brag. The BURNED draft hides the autonomy claim under
spy-vocab (`METHOD: AUTONOMOUS`), committing to neither the true story
nor the fiction. A costume with nothing underneath.

### 5. The human cost is the heart
*"The human's contribution? Direction. Ambition... and a truly
inadvisable amount of caffeine."* → *"He's fine."* → *"Probably."* That
self-deprecating button makes the awe affectionate. The BURNED draft has
attitude but no heart, because there's no human in it to love.

## ANTI-PATTERN: the abstraction layer that strangled transportation

The BURNED draft's most-engineered mechanism — the **R6 vocabulary
translation key** (turns "agents" → "autonomous field assets", "tests" →
"mission rehearsal artifacts", "code" → "operational tradecraft") — is
the exact machine that launders vivid truth into jargon. The cleverest
part of the build is what killed the transportation.

**Rule for the rebuild:** concrete true images beat in-world vocabulary
every time. If a spy-vocab translation removes a specific, visible,
human detail, the translation loses. Use the Pendleton flavor as
seasoning, never as a filter over the truth.

---

## Reuse vs. drop

**Reuse (the principle):** human-bet spine · concrete true images ·
escalation to an earned punchline · honest showcase framing · human cost
as the heart.

**Drop (UMB's surface — do NOT remake):**
- The noir narrator (Charon). BURNED has its own voice (Sterling-CODED).
- The literal SDLC-phase beat list (Thesis/Spec/Swarm/Code/Tests/Art).
- UMB's specific jokes ("He's fine. Probably." belongs to UMB).
- The mob-city / Secret-Hitler setting.

---

## Acceptance bar for the BURNED origin rebuild

The new beat sheet passes only if:
1. There is a **human protagonist** with a **bet** stated in the first
   ~10 seconds.
2. Every build/stat fact is delivered as a **concrete image**, not a
   jargon noun phrase. (Cold-read test: can a viewer *see* it?)
3. At least one sequence **escalates** to a **deadpan punchline** that a
   first-time viewer laughs at without context.
4. The autonomy claim is **owned honestly** at least once, not only
   smuggled through in-world chrome.
5. The **human cost** lands as a self-deprecating beat with heart.
6. It does **not** repeat any item on the Drop list.
