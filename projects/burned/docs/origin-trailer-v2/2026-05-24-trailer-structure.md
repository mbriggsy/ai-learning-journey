# Origin Trailer Structure — the spine

**Purpose:** The companion to
[`2026-05-24-origin-trailer-principles.md`](2026-05-24-origin-trailer-principles.md).
The principles doc is **the bar** (what makes an origin trailer land).
This is **the spine** (what shape the beats take). Use both: the spine
sequences the beats, the bar judges whether each one lands.

**This is directional structure, NOT a script.** It gives the beat
*frame* and the raw material to explore — never the locked lines, jokes,
or voice. (Same discipline as decisions-not-code: the shape is the
decision; the keystrokes are the brainstorm's job.)

---

## Frame: Story Circle, compressed to ~6 trailer beats

Dan Harmon's Story Circle (the Hero's-Journey distillation) compressed
from 8 steps into ~6 beats for a 90–106s origin trailer. The protagonist
is **the human (Briggsy)** — per the principles doc, the winning move is
a true human-bet story, not a feature briefing.

A trailer is **highlight extraction + emotional-peak sequencing**, not
scene-by-scene coverage. Pull the peaks; order them for pull.

| # | Story-Circle function | The beat's JOB | Raw material to explore (NOT locked) | Emotional peak target |
|---|----------------------|----------------|--------------------------------------|----------------------|
| 1 | **You** + **Need** (hook, first ~8s) | Drop us next to a person with a want. Earn attention instantly. | Enterprise engineer. A card game everyone loved at game night that deserved a screen. The itch. | Intrigue / recognition |
| 2 | **Go** (the bet) | State the dare out loud. This is the thesis. | A stack he'd never touched. A discipline he knew nothing about. The bet: *a machine could build something worth playing.* | Stakes / "wait, really?" |
| 3 | **Search** (the build) | The method, told as concrete vivid images that escalate. NOT a stat list. | The process: spec, the swarm, code, the test gauntlet. Concrete pictures (drunk-agent testing energy), not jargon. Escalation toward a release. | Rising tension → awe |
| 4 | **Find** (the proof) | The earned punchline. The "nothing broke" equivalent — tension, then deadpan snap. | The moment it WORKS. A first-time viewer should laugh without context. | Release / laugh |
| 5 | **Take** (the cost) | The human toll. The heart. Self-deprecating, honest. | What it cost the human (the caffeine/no-sleep register). The honest-flex beat lands somewhere here or in 6. | Warmth / affection |
| 6 | **Return** + **Change** (reveal + tag) | What came out the other side + the honest claim + a tag that sends them off. | The actual game people enjoy. The owned claim ("…that is the product" energy, BURNED-native). A closing tag/button. | Satisfaction → CTA |

---

## Rules baked into the spine

- **Hook in the first ~8 seconds.** Beat 1 has to grab before anyone
  swipes. No throat-clearing.
- **Concrete images, never jargon.** Beat 3 is where the old draft died
  (the R6 vocab key laundered truth into "contingencies war-gamed"). Pull
  the vivid true picture every time.
- **Exactly one earned punchline** (beat 4) with a real build under it.
- **The human cost is non-negotiable** (beat 5). It's the heart that
  keeps the autonomy claim from reading as a brag.
- **Own the flex honestly once** (beat 5 or 6), don't only smuggle it
  through chrome.
- **End on a tag**, not a fade. Send them off with a button.
