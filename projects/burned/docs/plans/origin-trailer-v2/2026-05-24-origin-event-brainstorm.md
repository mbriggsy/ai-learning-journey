### 1. The Performance Review
Janet assesses the bet like a withering agency file on the human.
> *"Subject: a man who builds enterprise software for a living. Objective: a spy game he had no business attempting. Method: he didn't write it. Any of it."*
- **Engine:** cold institutional assessment → a verdict.
- **Bar:** strong on human-protagonist + honest-flex. **Risk:** runs cold; the cost beat must carry the heart.

### 2. She Gave It Two Weeks  *(lean: safest knockout)*
Janet didn't think it'd work. The arc is her disbelief curdling into awe.
> *"I gave it a fortnight. Two weeks before he'd crawl back to his spreadsheets. …That was four hundred and eighty-seven tests ago."*
- **Engine:** her concession *is* the escalation; the punchline is the moment she caves. UMB's "I'm just impressed" DNA grown into a full spine.
- **Bar:** escalation + earned punchline + heart. **Risk:** low. Safest-strongest.

### 3. The Origin of Janet  *(lean: highest ceiling, highest risk)*
She narrates the build that conjured *her* into being.
> *"You want my origin story? A man made a bet. And not one of these words — including the ones I'm saying now — came from his hand."*
- **Engine:** self-referential genesis; the agency narrating its own creation. Most uniquely-BURNED.
- **Bar:** honest-flex is baked in. **Risk:** can tip into "wow, Claude built this" — only works if the bet stays the hero and Janet stays narrator.

### 4. Game Night, Translated
Open warm and true (friends, the card game everyone loved); Janet dryly recasts the mundane into spy-op grandeur.
> *"Every operation begins somewhere. This one began, as the great ones do, with snacks and a card game."*
- **Engine:** mundane-truth × spy-grandeur friction.
- **Bar:** concrete true images + warmth up front. **Risk:** the build must escalate or it deflates after the cozy open.

### 5. The Tell
The wager framed as a noir gamble: a man bets the impossible op, hands off the controls.
> *"Every operative has a tell. His was a confidence he hadn't earned. Yet."*
- **Engine:** stakes-forward; bet stated cold in the first breath.
- **Bar:** bet-up-front + tension. Clean and classic. **Risk:** low, but least distinctive.

### 6. The Disaster That Wasn't
Open *in media res* on apparent catastrophe (the test gauntlet — drunk agents, rage-tapping, disconnects); Janet narrates like it's collapsing… then the turn.
> *"I'd love to tell you it went smoothly. It did not. They were drunk. Disconnecting mid-vote. A catastrophe, by any measure. …It was supposed to be."*
- **Engine:** misdirect resolving into "this was the plan, and it held."
- **Bar:** escalation + earned punchline are structural. **Risk:** misdirect must resolve clean or a cold viewer is lost.

## Read / recommendation

- **#2** is the safest knockout — concession-as-escalation is the exact move that made UMB land; all heart and dry wit.
- **#3** has the highest ceiling and is the most *BURNED*, but flirts with the manifesto's "wow Claude built this" trap.
- **Possible fusion:** the skeptic who turns out to be the product — *"I gave it two weeks. …Then it built me."* (#2 spine + #3 reveal).
