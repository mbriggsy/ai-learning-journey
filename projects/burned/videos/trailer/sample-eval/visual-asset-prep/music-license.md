## Attribution text (REQUIRED)

This exact wording (or semantically equivalent) MUST appear wherever
the trailer is distributed:

> Music: "Spy Glass" by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com) — Licensed
> under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The CC-BY 4.0 deed requires:

1. **Attribution to creator** — "Kevin MacLeod"
2. **Title** — "Spy Glass"
3. **Source** — "incompetech.com"
4. **License indication** — "CC BY 4.0" or full name
5. **Link to the license** — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

These five elements can be combined in any order. The Incompetech
"how to attribute" page accepts the format above as valid.

uncluttered (no on-screen credit roll required).

## What CC-BY 4.0 allows

- ✅ Use for commercial purposes (portfolio recruiting, social
  media, engineering content)
- ✅ Adapt, remix, edit, clip the work (Phase 4 will clip Spy Glass
  to a 106s window from the 227s original)
- ✅ Combine with other content (trailer composites voice lines,
  visual chrome, R15 stamps)
- ✅ Distribute the resulting work on any platform

## What CC-BY 4.0 requires

- ✋ Provide attribution as above (every distribution surface)
- ✋ Indicate if changes were made — Phase 4's clipping qualifies
  as a change. Recommended addition to the attribution text: *"…
  edited from the original."* (optional but transparent)
- ✋ Don't apply additional restrictions that prevent others from
  using the licensed material in the same way (the trailer-as-
  composite is its own work; the LICENSE-SCOPE applies only to
  the Spy Glass track, not the whole trailer)
