## Landmines
- **Roth lever = calculator, NEVER a verdict.** Under Reg BI, a *recommendation* of an account type (Traditional-vs-Roth, rollovers) is treated as an "investment strategy involving securities"; a user-driven calculator isn't. The no-verdict + categorical-trigger + no-securities/no-asset-location guardrail is load-bearing, not stylistic. (findings §Strand 3)
- **Non-extractable key ≠ at-rest security boundary.** `extractable:false` stops *script* exfiltration, not disk access. The real protection is the encrypted blob + KDF. Don't let copy imply otherwise (gates R15). (findings §Strand 2)
- **Jazz is alpha** — don't build the v1 persistence/crypto core on it (2.0 alpha, new API). Single-device MVP likely needs no sync engine at all.
- **Engine validation numbers live in findings §Strand 4 ONLY** (avoid stat-drift). Watch the corrected ones: Trinity 100%-bond/4%/30yr = **~70%** (NOT 20–35%); Bengen 4.15% SAFEMAX = **1966** cohort (NOT 1968); MC runs *more pessimistic* than historical — assert a band, never equality. **cFIREsim can't reproduce Trinity exactly** (Shiller carries GOVERNMENT bonds; Trinity uses CORPORATE) → mode-A is **self-consistent (vs our own committed fixture) + directional** vs published numbers, NOT published-exact, until the corporate series is pinned.

- **Web served-JS caveat** — "we can't see your money" covers data at rest / in transit, NOT a malicious-code-update threat model. Disclose, don't overclaim (R15: provable before spoken).
- **No competitive lens** — product is the bar; don't reintroduce moat/defensibility framing.
- **DK key-hierarchy is LOCKED** (Briggsy's call 2026-06-03 over the scope-review's "simplify to flat"). Reason = **one data copy so recovery can never go stale** (flat = two copies that can drift → survivor restores a stale plan). Don't re-recommend flat. DK stays **non-exportable** — "wrap" via `encrypt()` of raw bytes, NEVER `wrapKey()` (forces extractable → XSS can export it).
- **In-memory model is the real XSS surface** — at-rest crypto guards the disk, NOT the cleartext model in JS memory while unlocked. A **strict CSP** (Unit 0, `vercel.json`) is load-bearing for "we can't see your money," separate from the disclosed served-JS caveat.
