# Glossary

The Back Nine has a dense private vocabulary — financial, statutory, and project-internal. This is the single place each term is defined, with a pointer to its canonical home. If a term is load-bearing enough to appear in more than one doc, it is defined here once and referenced elsewhere.

Sorted alphabetically; `§` and number-prefixed statutory terms are grouped at the end.

---

**ACA-MAGI** — the income measure that drives the pre-65 ACA premium credit: AGI + tax-exempt interest + the **full** (taxable *and* non-taxable) Social Security benefit + excluded foreign earned income. A *different* number from IRMAA-MAGI — the two are never reused for each other. → [architecture.md §7.2](architecture.md)

**ACA-PTC (Premium Tax Credit)** — the pre-65 marketplace subsidy that falls as ACA-MAGI rises. Modeled as a per-year fixed-point with an explicit 400%-FPL **cliff** branch. → [architecture.md §7.2](architecture.md), [research/pre65-healthcare.md](research/pre65-healthcare.md)

**Accumulation** — the pre-retirement saving phase (contributions + market growth) the engine projects forward to a candidate work-stop date. The mirror of decumulation; a **bounded near-retirement on-ramp**, never a FIRE calculator. → [plans/1-engine.md](plans/1-engine.md) (C2), [decisions/accumulation-fuck-off-date.md](decisions/accumulation-fuck-off-date.md)

**Act** — one of the four build chapters: **1 The Engine · 2 Where You Stand · 3 The Levers You Hold · 4 The Recommended Route**. The code comments say `P`n (Phase) for the same chapter; the globally-unique unit key is the join. → [roadmap.md → The ID scheme](roadmap.md#the-id-scheme)

**All-in cost (pay-less-tax objective)** — the quantity the *pay less tax* goal ranks on and its hero delta renders: per path, lifetime income tax (federal + any priced state layer) + the ACA premium after the PTC + Medicare (base Part B + IRMAA + the Part D / Medigap / MA extras), real dollars, the MEAN over paths of that per-path sum, lower is better. RULED 2026-10-05 by Briggsy ("All-in cost"); composed in ONE function, `lifetimeAllInCostPerPath` (`src/engine/solver/objectiveHeadline.ts`). The hero's words live in the `recDeltaPayLessTax` slot (`src/ui/copy.ts`). ⚑ Three other things in the product say "all-in" and are NOT this: **(1)** the Medicare "all-in" figure — base Part B + the Part D / Medigap extras, the health sheet's premium-card framing (`docs/council-log.md` 2026-09-13 row; the `taxOverlay.ts:2722` comment; the `medicareExtrasAmountLabel` "Monthly premium, all in") — Medicare only, no tax, no ACA premium; **(2)** the U11 regime-toggle preview's lifetime healthcare cost — the MEDIAN over paths of net ACA premium + Medicare (`roth.ts` `readingFrom`, the `lifetimeNetPremiumReal` docblock in `src/shared/model.ts`) — premiums only, no income tax, and a median, not a mean; **(3)** "Household spending, all in" — the intake's spending label (`spendLabel`; `docs/council-log.md` 2026-07-11 row, its income-tax boundary) — what the household spends, not what it pays in tax or premiums. → [plans/4-recommendation.md → contract #4](plans/4-recommendation.md#the-eight-cross-cutting-contracts), [architecture.md §7.5](architecture.md#75-the-solver-output-contract-m6)

**already-failing / over-funded / on-track / borderline / off-track / indeterminate** — the closed set of **outcome states** the engine owns; the headline reading and copy are selected from these, never from a 7th ad-hoc state. → [plans/1-engine.md](plans/1-engine.md) (U1)

**Batched-oracle law** — Briggsy's 2026-07-11 grant for the Caddie: a chaired verdict card SHIPS by default (**PILOT-CLEARED**), with no per-surface park; his eye audits at product cadence and always supersedes. One-way doors, framing-level forks and genuine pilot doubt still park (**PARKED-FOR-BRIGGSY**). A card cannot clear while a finding that questions whether a shown figure, date or claim actually holds stays open: it holds at **HARD-FLAG — HELD FOR THE ORACLE** until the engine's measurement or a build closes the finding, then turns PILOT-CLEARED (the skill's holds rule). → [caddie/cold-read-log.md](caddie/cold-read-log.md), [.claude/skills/caddie/SKILL.md](../.claude/skills/caddie/SKILL.md)

**Bengen / SAFEMAX** — the safe-withdrawal-rate validation anchor (the "4% rule" lineage). The engine validates a duration-conservative SAFEMAX-analogue (~3.67%) against a committed Damodaran Treasury proxy arm, not against a bit-exact dataset. → [plans/1-engine.md](plans/1-engine.md) (U1), [research/engine-validation-and-tax.md](research/engine-validation-and-tax.md)

**Box-Muller** — the transform that turns uniforms into normal market draws. Kept **stateless** (no cached spare across calls) so two CRN candidates that draw in different interleavings never silently desync. → [architecture.md §2](architecture.md)

**Bridge (earned-income bridge)** — the cash term that nets the year's withdrawal **down** by the money coming in: `net = max(0, spending − earned − ongoing income − Social Security)` in `cashTermsForYear` (`src/engine/simulate.ts`); never credits a dead earner, never contributes surplus back. Its pay term can lower a draw only in a year the **working-year clamp** does not fire, so only on a run without the accumulation construct. Every date-route candidate carries the construct (`buildCandidateParams`, `src/engine/dateSearch.ts`), so there a working year's spending draw is the clamp's 0, and with **W** answered the **pay check** draws any shortfall on top. *(Corrected 2026-10-30 (b9-8) by the pay check's U11: this entry gave the formula as `max(0, spending − earnedIncome)` and called the bridge the working-years transform.)* → [architecture.md §4](architecture.md)

**byte-identity** — see **reduce-to-spine**.

**Caddie** — the Briggsy-proxy cold reader: walks the real rendered UI at his 1536×791 laptop viewport and on phone, fans out fresh-context readers calibrated on his taste corpus, hunts the calm-but-wrong reading, and files a chaired verdict card. A subordinate stand-in, never his N=1 read — every prediction is scored against his eye on [the tape](caddie/tape.md). → [caddie/cold-read-log.md](caddie/cold-read-log.md)

**Cardinal rule** — *"calm-but-wrong is the sin."* The governing constraint on every engine decision, disclosure, and the voice itself; the bar **rises** for a recommender. → [product.md §2](product.md)

**Cohort tables** — the SSA TR2024/Alt2 per-birth-year survival tables; the household's **1969 / 1972** cohorts drive joint-and-survivor longevity (not a period table, not a Gompertz fit). → [plans/1-engine.md](plans/1-engine.md) (U1)

**copyGuard** — the lint enforcing calm, honest language: a **ban-list** (certainty verbs, catastrophe lexicon) plus the **require-the-hedge** positive lint, both reading one single-sourced token catalog. → [product.md §6](product.md), [plans/3-controls.md](plans/3-controls.md) (U10)

**Council of Elders** — the `/council` mechanism: diverse expert agents debate a non-trivial judgment call in fresh context windows, are adversarially verified, and a chair returns a confidence-graded, tier-classified recommendation — convened instead of parking the call on Briggsy. The **Honesty Hawk** seat (calm-but-wrong is the sin) sits on every council and holds a **veto**. → [council-log.md](council-log.md), [.claude/skills/council/SKILL.md](../.claude/skills/council/SKILL.md)

**CRN (Common Random Numbers)** — every candidate strategy, date-offset, and arm sees **identical** market draws, so a difference between them is signal, not RNG luck. The load-bearing determinism contract that lets the solver rank candidates honestly. → [architecture.md §2–§3](architecture.md)

**Damodaran arm** — the committed Treasury / corporate total-return series used as the independent Bengen/Trinity validation proxy (no canonical bit-exact dataset exists). → [research/engine-validation-and-tax.md](research/engine-validation-and-tax.md)

**Date-search** — the bounded, **exhaustive, non-monotone-robust** sweep over the household work-stop offset `Y` that produces the fuck-off date; reads off a quantized conservative lower confidence bound, never a bisection. Each track yields one of **three first-class outcomes**: a **confirmed** date (a candidate below the window top with later-offset evidence), a **window-edge** date (the window top, reported with the unconfirmed-tail disclosure, never silently crowned), or **no-date-in-window** (a first-class result, never "never free," never a crash); `Y == 0` reads *"work-optional at today."* → [plans/1-engine.md](plans/1-engine.md) (C3), [decisions/accumulation-fuck-off-date.md](decisions/accumulation-fuck-off-date.md) §3

**Decumulation** — the retirement draw-down phase; the engine's center of gravity (accumulation exists to solve for the date that hands off to it). → [plans/1-engine.md](plans/1-engine.md)

**Delta-as-hero** — the rule for comparing two futures: the *difference* leads as the hero figure (*"in 8 of 10 futures instead of 6"*) and the baseline is shown honestly but secondary — Act 3's `TwoFutures` controls, and Act 4's **surplus pivot**. → [plans/3-controls.md](plans/3-controls.md) (U10)

**directional-until-pinned** — a constants-table marker: a figure is provisional until confirmed against its named government/issuer primary at a pin pass (`directionalUntilPinned: false`). → [architecture.md §8](architecture.md)

**DK (data key)** — the random key that encrypts the model exactly **once**; the passphrase-derived and recovery-derived keys each wrap it independently, so the recovery path can never restore a stale copy. → [architecture.md §7.3](architecture.md)

**DND/012 (externally-derived fixtures)** — golden expected values must be derived by an **independent** path (hand-math, a published figure), never via the engine's own formula (which would prove typing, not correctness). → [architecture.md §5](architecture.md)

**DRC (delayed retirement credits)** — Social Security increases for claiming after FRA. For a survivor base they are **realized at the deceased's death**, never credited for years the worker did not live to earn. → [architecture.md §7.7](architecture.md)

**Earnings test (the Social Security earnings test)** — how much Social Security the run actually pays in a year when someone under full retirement age works while a benefit is due to them or on their record, applied only on a run carrying **W** (`earningsTestSchedule`, `src/engine/earningsTest.ts`, one schedule per path, built on that path's own deaths). In a year wholly before the **FRA** year it holds back $1 for every $2 of the year's gross pay above the lower exempt amount; in the FRA year, $1 for every $3 of the pre-FRA months' pay above the higher one (the amounts live in `src/engine/constants/workingYears.ts`, never re-typed here). Pay only — never pensions, investment income or Social Security — and a survivor is tested on the survivor's own pay, never the deceased's. A month held back in a year certainly before full retirement age, both the earner's and the payee's (survivor FRA for a widow(er)'s benefit), raises that benefit later; a month held back in a year that may hold FRA (the FRA year's stopgap among them) or from a payee already past FRA raises nothing. The raise lands on own and spousal benefits after the payee's full retirement age and on a widow(er)'s at 62 and at survivor FRA, and the deceased's held-back months raise the **RIB-LIM** cap. Under W the schedule's paid figure is the year's one Social Security number — cash, taxable benefits and IRMAA-MAGI all read it — so a held-back benefit is never taxed. Until the birth month is asked, the FRA year is a stopgap: it holds back the most that year can and credits none of it (gloomy), while a claim made at FRA, or a widow(er) benefit begun that year, is paid the whole year (rosy) (KTD11). Months held back before the plan's first year are invisible and never credited (gloomy). → [architecture.md §7.7](architecture.md), [plans/features/working-year-pay-check-build-spec.md](plans/features/working-year-pay-check-build-spec.md) (R12–R15, KTD10–KTD11)

**Externally-derived fixtures** — see **DND/012**.

**FRA (full retirement age)** — the Social Security age at which the unreduced PIA is paid. → [architecture.md §7.7](architecture.md)

**Fuck-off date** — the working/product name for the not-yet-retired household's first answer: *"when is work optional?"*, delivered as **two confidence-graded dates** (floor + lifestyle). The user-facing label holds the calm advisor voice. → [product.md §3](product.md), [decisions/accumulation-fuck-off-date.md](decisions/accumulation-fuck-off-date.md)

**Gross-up** — the tax overlay's bounded fixed-point: raise the withdrawal enough to cover spending **plus** the tax on that withdrawal (the worst-case contraction factor `k` and `GROSS_UP_MAX_PASSES` are pinned ONCE in architecture §7.1 — never re-typed here). → [architecture.md §7.1](architecture.md)

**HARD-FLAG** — see **Batched-oracle law**.

**Hedge (the hedge on the headline)** — every recommendation/control readout must carry its probabilistic qualifier **on the primary surface**, never buried in tapped-away math. Enforced by the require-the-hedge lint. → [product.md §6](product.md)

**Held-out seed (seed-set A / B)** — the optimizer's-curse defense: the solver **selects** on seed-set **A** (the scenario's persisted seed) and **grades and displays** on an independent seed-set **B**, derived from A by `deriveSeedB` (a SplitMix expansion, never `seedA + 1`) and never independently persisted — the derivation from A is the truth (a saved recommendation stores it only for byte-reproducibility). The Tier-1 survival tie-tolerance is decided A-side, never on a B-measured band, and its live value is an exact **0** by ruling ([decisions/survival-tie-tolerance.md](decisions/survival-tie-tolerance.md), council `wf_6e72eca9-c20`, 2026-10-06); any future loosening stays CRN-difference-keyed and must clear that record's reopen gates first. → [plans/4-recommendation.md → contract #2](plans/4-recommendation.md#the-eight-cross-cutting-contracts)

**Honesty Hawk** — see **Council of Elders**.

**HSA** — the triple-advantaged, **medical-earmarked** fourth account bucket. Covers out-of-pocket + (owner 65+) Medicare premiums tax-free — **not** ACA marketplace premiums. Never a general drawdown source. → [architecture.md §7.2](architecture.md)

**ID scheme (R / U / C / D / M / §)** — the compact, stable internal join-keys (Requirement / Unit / unit-tracks / Milestone / Section) the docs and code share; defined once, never shown to the user, never renumbered. Canonical legend: [roadmap.md → The ID scheme](roadmap.md#the-id-scheme). (product.md §4's D1–D7 are locked product decisions, not unit tracks — same letter, unrelated.)

**INCOME_TYPES / COLA_MODES** — the R40 other-income vocabulary, single-sourced as `const` arrays (in `src/shared/model.ts`) that the intake and the restore codec both import: `INCOME_TYPES` = pension / rental / alimony / annuity / other; `COLA_MODES` = real-flat / nominal-flat / fixed-pct. → [product.md](product.md) (R40.1–R40.2)

**Insight & cross-repo citation IDs** — a numbered, hard-won engineering lesson, cited by **full relative path** (`docs/insights/NNN-…`; a bare in-repo number is acceptable shorthand where the index resolves it — sibling-project lessons ALWAYS carry their full path). The full-path rule + the `burned`/`DND`/`AJS` shorthand decoder: [insights/README.md](insights/README.md).

**IRMAA** — the post-65 Medicare premium **surcharge**, set on a **2-year-lagged** MAGI lookback, with hard per-person step-cliffs. → [architecture.md §7.2](architecture.md)

**IRMAA-MAGI** — IRMAA's income measure: AGI + tax-exempt interest, with **no** Social Security add-back. A *different* number from ACA-MAGI. → [architecture.md §7.2](architecture.md)

**K1–K15 (the pay check's gap keys)** — internal labels, never shown, for the open gaps that can move the **pay check**; each is one line (one or two sentences) in the `working-year-tax` methodology row's W arm (`workingCostLineKeys` in `METHODOLOGY_DISCLOSURES`, read through `methodologyLines`, `src/ui/assumptionRegistry.ts`) that names its lean in plain words, in a pinned order. *Looks better than it really is:* K1 no 10% early-withdrawal charge on a short year drawn before 59½; K2 self-employment (only an employee's half of payroll tax); K3 the §219(g) IRA-deduction phase-out; K4 the Roth-only catch-up; K5 a benefit begun at full retirement age paid that whole year. *Could go either way:* K6 pay as one steady figure; K7 the state you work in (shown only where the run taxes working years by the retirement state itself, NC's and FL's readings); K8 the wage base and the earnings test's exempt amounts held flat in today's dollars. *Looks a little worse:* K9 left-over pay not saved; K10 the FRA-year stopgap; K11 health premiums and HSA money taken from pay before tax; K12 a job outside Social Security; K13 months held back before the plan's first year; K15 a retired spouse's share of the plan at work kept in **W** after her Medicare starts. K14 is retired (the RIB-LIM read settled it). A gap gets a key only while its register entry is open and it can move the check, and the build that closes it deletes the key. Not the budget's `k` (years since the work stop) or the gross-up's `k`. → [plans/features/working-year-pay-check-build-spec.md](plans/features/working-year-pay-check-build-spec.md) (U9), [backlog.md](backlog.md)

**KTD (Known Technical Decision)** — a load-bearing build decision, numbered so the implementation can cite it (e.g. R40's nine KTDs), recorded in its decision record ([docs/decisions/other-income-r40.md](decisions/other-income-r40.md)).

**Lexicographic objective** — the definition of "best": **Tier 1** = full-plan survival on the retired route (`Distribution.survivalFraction`, the headline's own statistic), never traded for the goal; then **Tier 2** a user-chosen surplus goal (*leave more · pay less tax · live bigger now*; *pay less tax* ranks on **all-in cost**, see that entry). On the retired route Tier 1 ranks on the hero's own field, so the objective and the headline cannot name different quantities; the count shown can differ (seed A decides, seed B displays). The essentials floor is reported, never ranked — see **Survival vs the floor**. The date-route key is ruled with the working-route solve. ⚑ *The D1 amendment of 2026-10-12 (council `wf_cfa961da-13c`), RATIFIED by Briggsy 2026-10-12.* → [product.md](product.md) (decision D1 / R21)

**MAGI** — modified adjusted gross income. The Back Nine computes **two distinct** MAGIs — see **ACA-MAGI** and **IRMAA-MAGI** — never one shared number.

**Method C (POMS)** — the Social Security spousal computation: the spouse's **own benefit in full plus a reduced excess**, *not* `max(own, spousal)`. (`H` = the higher earner, `argmax(pia)`; `L` = the lower earner who can claim a spousal excess on `H`'s record; `BenefitPerson` is the per-person record the sub-engine consumes.) → [architecture.md §7.7](architecture.md)

**MFJ→single** — the surviving spouse's filing-status switch the year after the first death: the same real dollars fall into ~half-width single brackets — the "tax cliff" that is the recommendation's emotional headline. → [architecture.md §7.1](architecture.md)

**Money picture (the working-year money picture)** — the setup step after the W question ("How a working year adds up") and the assumption panel's W row: year 0 of the **pay check** in the household's own numbers, under Coming in, Going out, the bottom line ("Left over" or "Short", with a sign) and Also paid from savings (Medicare only, never netted into the sum), the lines summing exactly to the bottom line. Then every later working year in which a discrete change lands for this household (a claim or a spousal start, an earnings-test step, a Medicare start, turning 65, a catch-up step-down, an income stream starting or ending, a pinned law step) or whose own bottom line is short, each worded "If you’re still working in …" (a stretch of consecutive short years with no new event after its first reads as one line, "If you’re still working from … through …"). One engine producer, `workingYearLedger` (`src/engine/workingYearLedger.ts`), composed on the main thread from the same functions the run's year loop calls (an import allowlist pins it, and year 0 is pinned to the overlay's own year 0); intake reads it through `workingYearLedgerForRun` (`src/intake/intakeMap.ts`). It reads the no-death timeline at the window-top candidate and never reads a crown. → [plans/features/working-year-pay-check-build-spec.md](plans/features/working-year-pay-check-build-spec.md) (R16, KTD12)

**No-change state** — the recommendation's honest arm (R25) when the crowned winner is the strategy the household already runs, compared by plan (`noChange` in `src/engine/solver/select.ts`, decided A-side at selection, never on a seed-B band): the second beat says *"You're already on one of the strongest paths we tested"* instead of manufacturing an active move; U14's oracle case (v) pins it. → [plans/4-recommendation.md → Unit 16](plans/4-recommendation.md#unit-16--recommendation-surface)

**Odds ladder** — the date route's discrete per-offset chart, always on display beside the band for a dated hero (it withdraws only when an aged plan's crowned date has already passed): one dot per evaluated work-stop offset on whole "X of 10" rungs, showing how the hero date's work-optional odds shift by *when* you stop (`src/viz/OddsLadder.tsx`). The 2026-07-03 N=1 cold-read superseded the council's original one-pull-down drawer. → [plans/2-first-answer.md](plans/2-first-answer.md) (unit D2)

**Oracle-cleared token** — the branded `OracleClearedToken` (`src/engine/validation/oracleToken.ts`) that only the U14 validation harness can mint. It is withheld while any of its clauses fails, among them the optimality oracle, ranking stability, a calibrated ε, and no certification-pinnable figure the run consumes still directional (methodology-substrate figures ship disclosed and never block). `solve()` takes it as a **required parameter** — recommending without validation is a compile error — and refuses a token minted over a different **solver-run fingerprint**. → [plans/4-recommendation.md → contract #1](plans/4-recommendation.md#the-eight-cross-cutting-contracts), [architecture.md §8](architecture.md)

**Outcome states** — see **already-failing / over-funded / …**.

**Overlay** — a **zero-draw** deterministic transform of the cash-flow term (the earned-income bridge, tax, healthcare, accumulation inflow, and, under **W**, the pay check's shortfall) fed into the same per-year update function as the spine; each reduces to the spine when off. → [architecture.md §4–§5](architecture.md)

**Pay check (the working-year pay check)** — the money check every working year runs once the household has answered **W** (live since `c07e5758`, 2026-10-30). In each year the **working-year clamp** holds, `workingYearCashLines` (`src/engine/taxOverlay.ts`) adds the living workers' gross pay, Social Security as the **earnings test** pays it, and other income, then takes out **payroll tax**, the income tax the pay, the other income and the entered investment income add on top of what Social Security alone owes (read off the **working-year tax floor**'s one preparation; Social Security's own tax, T(0, ss), is still paid from savings, and a state tax on pay the tool does not work out, PA's or an unpriced state's, rides inside W), the household's own savings deposits (the employer match and the employer's HSA dollar taken out: `ownDepositsForYear`, `src/engine/simulate.ts`) and that year's W. A negative bottom line is the shortfall, added to the year's need and drawn from savings through the household's withdrawal order, the same on both tracks. A positive one is not saved (K9; build 2, the surplus question, follows). The entered investment income is taxed in the check but never counted as money in, because the portfolio's return already holds it. Without W every run is byte-identical to the build before the check; the spine route never carries W, and the recommendation runs only on that route. → [architecture.md §7.4](architecture.md), [plans/features/working-year-pay-check-build-spec.md](plans/features/working-year-pay-check-build-spec.md) (R5–R11)

**Payroll tax (as modeled)** — the employee's federal Social Security and Medicare tax on a working year's pay, charged only inside the **pay check** (`payrollTaxForYear`, `src/engine/payrollTax.ts`): Social Security tax on each worker's pay up to the wage base, Medicare tax on all of it, and the extra Medicare tax on the return's combined pay above its filing-status line, a dollar figure the law never indexes, so the engine deflates it by its one price index. Wages are gross pay: 401(k), 403(b) and 457(b) deferrals stay in, and so, until intake asks, do health premiums and cafeteria-plan HSA money taken before tax (K11). The employee's half only, so a self-employed worker is under-charged (K2); a job outside Social Security is still charged (K12). It never lowers the income-tax base. The rates and lines live in `src/engine/constants/workingYears.ts`; the wage base is held flat in today's dollars, though the law moves it with average wages (K8). → [architecture.md §7.4](architecture.md), [plans/features/working-year-pay-check-build-spec.md](plans/features/working-year-pay-check-build-spec.md) (R8, KTD9)

**PIA (Primary Insurance Amount)** — the Social Security benefit at FRA; the per-person SS input (entered monthly, stored annually). → [architecture.md §7.7](architecture.md)

**PILOT-CLEARED** — see **Batched-oracle law**.

**Presence-keyed** — byte-identity keyed on a construct's **absence** from params, not its zero value: a zero-valued-but-constructed accumulation run is deliberately *not* byte-identical (its working-year clamp is live). **W** is keyed the same way: the engine runs the pay check on a W of 0, though intake never carries one. → [architecture.md §5](architecture.md), [decisions/accumulation-fuck-off-date.md](decisions/accumulation-fuck-off-date.md) §1

**reduce-to-spine** — the core correctness invariant: every overlay, when **off**, reproduces the Trinity/Bengen-validated decumulation distribution **byte-identically** (same seed). The golden cases are never perturbed. → [architecture.md §5](architecture.md)

**Recovery passphrase** — a **second user-chosen memorable passphrase** (PBKDF2-600k, same strength floor as the daily one; `firstSave` rejects recovery == daily) that wraps the DK independently of the daily passphrase; the surviving spouse's primary door back into the vault. Mandatory export at first save. Superseded the v1 system-minted BIP-39 12-word phrase (U8 rework, council 2026-06-30 — the phrase was DOA for a non-technical audience). The UI calls it the **recovery word** (`copy.ts` `recoveryHeading` / `recoveryLabel`); product.md R17 calls it the recovery credential. → [architecture.md §7.3](architecture.md)

**Recovery word** — the UI's name for the **Recovery passphrase** (see it).

**Register tiers (Tier 0–4)** — the open register's severity scale, by worst consequence: **Tier 0** calm-but-wrong (shipped code can answer wrong) · **Tier 1** the differentiator does not land · **Tier 2** what breaks on someone else's device · **Tier 3** Briggsy's call (taste, scope, one-way doors) · **Tier 4** hygiene (no user-visible wrong answer). → [backlog.md](backlog.md)

**reVerifyEveryBuild** — a constants-entry flag for live, possibly-retroactive policy that CI re-verifies at every build: the ACA enhanced-subsidy entries (`pnpm verify:aca`), NC's state-tax record (`pnpm verify:state-tax`), and the Social Security spousal rate (a dated tripwire test). → [architecture.md §8](architecture.md)

**RIB-LIM** — the Retirement-Insurance-Benefit limit that caps a Social Security survivor benefit. → [architecture.md §7.7](architecture.md)

**Survival vs the floor (vocabulary rule)** — two words, two quantities, never swapped. **Survival** (and **Tier 1**, on the retired route) = full-plan survival: the share of futures in which the WHOLE plan, essentials + discretionary, was funded every year — `Distribution.survivalFraction`, the hero's X of 10, the quantity the solver ranks. **The floor** = the essentials-only reading (`FloorTrack`, present only when a budget rides): the counterfactual where the household spends only essentials on the same draws, from year 0 on the retired route. On the date route the essentials trim starts only once no living member still works (the stop date): a working year's spending draw is the clamp's 0 on both tracks, and under the **pay check** (W answered) the floor's working years charge the same untrimmed **W** as the full plan and draw the same shortfall (the council's product-R27 reading, shipped flagged: the pay check's KTD19). It is reported — the relief line and the floor date — and never ranked, and the full-plan count is no lower bound on it (R27's 100%-FPL inversion can put the floor below the full track). With no budget the two coincide. "Survival floor" fuses the two words; for the essentials reading write "the essentials floor". R2's requirement ("separates survival from lifestyle") and its example both predate the rule and use "survival" for the essentials; read both as the floor (R2 carries a dated ⚑ note saying so). Ruled 2026-10-12 (council `wf_cfa961da-13c`, ⚑ the D1 amendment RATIFIED by Briggsy 2026-10-12). → [product.md](product.md) (D1 / R21 / R27)

**Survivor-spending ratio** — the factor scaling a survivor's spending after the first death (~75%, grounded to the Blanchett literature, directional-until-pinned). It scales the survivor's spend on both tracks, so it rides Tier 1's full-plan survival and the reported essentials floor alike, and its dangerous direction (too low) is documented. It never scales the household's own entered out-of-pocket medical figure, which is held whole on both spend arms — a disclosed conservative lean (council 2026-10-08), not a research finding. Under the **pay check** it also scales the part of a survivor's working-year figure above its held-whole sticky part (see **W**). → [plans/1-engine.md](plans/1-engine.md) (U1) · [decisions/ss-computation.md §6a](decisions/ss-computation.md)

**W (what a working year costs)** — the household's answer to "What does a year cost while you’re still working?", the **pay check**'s one new setup answer, yearly in today's dollars. It counts what the household spends, health premiums for anyone not yet on Medicare (taken from pay or bought directly), the Medicare premiums of anyone on Medicare who still works, local taxes on pay, state disability or family-leave deductions, and any state income tax on pay the tool does not work out (PA's, or an unpriced state's). It leaves out what the tool works out itself: federal payroll and income tax, savings deposits, Medicare premiums for anyone who has stopped working, and NC's income tax on pay (the W help, the `workingCostHelp…` keys in `src/ui/copy.ts`). Saved as the top-level `workingYearCost` (`WorkingYearCostV3`, `src/shared/model.ts`), optional — it landed additive within schemaVersion 3, and it is the field the 2026-10-31 bump to version 4 exists for (a copy built before the pay check ignored W, so a current save now carries a version that copy refuses; [architecture.md](architecture.md) §7.3) — in one of two kinds: **confirmed**, a yes to the quote (`workingYearQuote`, `src/intake/workingYearQuote.ts`: the spending figure, or with a budget its first-year lines plus the out-of-pocket medical figure, any discretionary line that ends or starts later left out and named), or **entered**, a figure of the household's own with the period it was typed in. Both keep the state-tax reading it was given under (`workingYearStateTaxReading`, read from the draft's retirement state). It goes **stale** when a confirmed answer's quote moves or either kind's state-tax reading moves (`workingYearCostStaleness`, the one predicate every surface calls): the engine keeps using the last figure given, the walk cannot finish on it, the result carries a standing line, and the unlock gate names it as an input to re-check, never a rules change. Intake requires W on the date route through the W step's gate (the sanity rule `working-cost-unanswered`), never through `missingRequiredFacts`, so a provisional date shown before the W step is answered runs without it, and so does the next one after a household clears its W at the W step, goes back and continues; the codec keeps it optional. On a survivor path a working year costs W_t = s + r·(W − s), s = min(W, S): S, the survivor-sticky part of a working year (a budget's first-year sticky lines with the medical figure, or the medical figure alone), is held whole and the rest scales by the **survivor-spending ratio** r (`workingYearCostForYear`, `src/engine/simulate.ts`). In the engine it is `accumulation.workingYearCostReal`, presence-keyed. → [plans/features/working-year-pay-check-build-spec.md](plans/features/working-year-pay-check-build-spec.md) (R1–R4, R9, KTD3–KTD5, KTD18, KTD21)

**Working-year clamp (the §7 clamp)** — the date route's working-year rule ([decisions/accumulation-fuck-off-date.md](decisions/accumulation-fuck-off-date.md) §7), single-sourced in `cashTermsForYear` (`src/engine/simulate.ts`): while the accumulation construct is present and any living member still works, the year's spending draw is 0 and the deposits stay as entered. Presence-keyed, never value-keyed, and death-aware: once the last living worker dies, the clamp stops firing and the survivor draws normally. It assumes pay covers the year. With **W** answered the **pay check** tests that and draws any shortfall on top; a run without W keeps the bare clamp: the walk's provisional date before the W step is answered, and again after a household clears its W at the W step and goes back; engine tests; and, from the first real save on, a copy of the app built before the pay check (since U10 every date seed and plant carries W). Savings still pays some things in a clamped year — a forced RMD, a planned conversion, a retired spouse's Medicare and, under W, any shortfall — and the **working-year tax floor** taxes them on top of the pay. → [architecture.md §4](architecture.md)

**Working-year pay check** — see **Pay check**.

**Working-year tax floor** — how a clamped working year taxes what savings still pays out (2026-10-20, [plans/features/working-year-tax-floor-build-spec.md](plans/features/working-year-tax-floor-build-spec.md)): the portfolio pays only the tax its own income adds on top of the year's outside income, plus the tax Social Security alone carries. The outside income is each worker's pay less their own pre-tax deferral, the taxable part of the clamped other income, and the entered working-year investment income, which is read three ways (left out, as ordinary income, as dividends and gains) with the highest charge kept (`prepareWorkingYearFloor` and `workingYearFederalCharge`, `src/engine/taxOverlay.ts`). The **pay check** reads its tax line off the same prepared floor. Not the essentials floor (see **Survival vs the floor**). → [architecture.md §7.4](architecture.md)

**Working-years vintage** — the stamp on the payroll and earnings-test figures, `WORKING_YEARS_VINTAGE` (`src/engine/constants/workingYears.ts`): a string bumped on any value change (a year stamp could not move on an in-year correction without tripping the annual-roll tripwire), bound to the table's content by a digest test. Dev plant: `?vault=wyclock`. → [architecture.md §8](architecture.md)
