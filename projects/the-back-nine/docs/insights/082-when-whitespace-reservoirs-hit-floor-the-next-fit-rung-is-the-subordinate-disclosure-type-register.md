## Problem

The extras unit grew the priced-Medicare pair again: the affirmation now names extra coverage,
and the on-typical frames append the per-person bi-directional sentence INSIDE the residual
paragraph. The one-frame law (`--laptop-fit-height` 800, the R13 disclaimer protected) was
already living at the bottom of its whitespace budget — rounds 1–3 (the U13 saga + the Medicare
pricing unit) had stepped the note-frame row-gaps to their sanctioned floor and the pair's
leading from body 1.55 to snug 1.4. The grown pair needed ~40–50px the frame did not have, and
the degrade law forbids the obvious lever: content is never cut to fit (Briggsy's law, memory
`content-outranks-aesthetics`).

## Root Cause

The fit-degrade ladder had only two named rungs (whitespace, then leading), and both were
spent. Without a third rung, the next pressure event forces either a content cut (forbidden), a
hero-scale change (dishonest — the verdict must dominate), or a protected-element fold breach
(the Hawk's veto). The ladder needed a rung that buys real pixels while keeping every word and
the frame's honesty hierarchy intact.

## Fix

**Round 4:** the medicare pair stepped into the R13 disclaimer's OWN subordinate-disclosure
register — `--text-sm`, the SAME register, the same frame, long-cleared by his eye — worth
~3px/line plus ~12% more glyphs per line ≈ 40–50px on the on-typical frames, words untouched.
TWO-PANE ONLY: the phone column scrolls freely and keeps the pair at base, byte-identical.
**Round 5** (CI 29176753170): ubuntu's taller metrics wrap the on-typical pair one line past
Windows, so the payment re-ran as platform-independent arithmetic SCOPED to exactly the frames
carrying the appended sentence (`.cs-medicare-residual--typical`: chain row-gaps → 2px, leading
snug → tight), while entered/mixed frames keep round 4 verbatim. Every rung ships as a
COMPUTED-STYLE pin that goes red on every platform if the rule is deleted (insight 075's
standing encoding); the leading pin branches on the modifier so both regimes stay pinned.

## Key Insight

The one-frame degrade ladder is ORDERED, and each rung has its own law: (1) whitespace —
gaps/padding, tier-scoped; (2) leading — body → snug → tight, arithmetic provable per wrapped
line; (3) **the TYPE REGISTER of subordinate disclosures** — and the register rung is honest
exactly BECAUSE it borrows a register an equal-rank subordinate ALREADY wears in the same frame
(the R13 disclaimer's `--text-sm`): the disclosure remains visibly subordinate, hierarchy is
preserved, and no novel size enters the system. Shrinking a HERO, minting a new smaller size,
or cutting words are all still forbidden — the ladder buys pixels from density, never from
meaning. And every rung, whatever its mechanism, is pinned by computed style so the fix is
falsifiable on the platform where it ISN'T needed (075: a green local run carries zero
information about the failing regime).
