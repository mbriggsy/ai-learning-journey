## Problem

The health sheet's premium card quoted `medicareAnchor` — the FIRST quotable year in which a base Part B bill landed — as "In your plan's Medicare years, premiums could run about ~$2,700 a year for your household." On `healthnc` (61/59 today) that year has ONE enrollee; two years later both are enrolled and the base is ~$5,800. One year's true figure, worded as the era: rosier than true on the number a couple repeats aloud (the sheets walk's Card 8, BLOCKER).

The obvious fix — the register's own prescription, and the opening position of seven of nine council seats — was copy-only: find "the first year both are enrolled" from the draft's AGES (`currentAge + yearsIn ≥ 65`, the same predicate the step card beneath already used for its two-of-you fork) and "single-source it with the step card so the two cards can never drift." It would have shipped a NEW instance of the same defect on the not-yet-retired half, and the "single-source" instinct would have propagated the fork rather than closed it.

## Root Cause

Two, one on each side of the wire.

**The engine decides enrollment; the UI re-derived it from the engine's inputs.** `resolveYear` prices Medicare on `medicareEnrolledCount` — the living set ∩ {member's year ≥ onset}, where onset is `max(65 − currentAge, retireOffset)` (`healthcareStreams.ts`): a spouse working past 65 is NOT enrolled and NOT billed, a dead spouse is never billed. The engine's own comment says the companion count, `count65`, "stays BIOLOGICAL" — it exists for the §63(f) deduction stack, a tax fact, not a pricing fact. The sheet's `currentAge + yearsIn ≥ 65` was `count65` wearing the pricing count's clothes: it agreed with the engine on every seed that had shipped (all retired, nobody working past 65, nobody dead on the median path) and disagreed exactly where the not-yet-retired expansion (R26–R39) sends households. An "era year" asserted from ages would have quoted a ONE-enrolled base under "once you're both on Medicare" — Card 8's 2× reproduced BY the fix. Insight 081's rule at the level of a decision rather than an early return: sharing a predicate with the producer is proxy-by-derivation, and the guards the producer carries (onset, death, work) are precisely what the mirror does not know.

**The wire carried no count, so the UI HAD to proxy.** `HealthReadoutYear` reported the base median, the surcharge median and the extras median, and nothing about WHO they were billed on. A readout that reports a household figure without its denominator forces every consumer to invent one.

Two more findings the red team made at source, each a smaller instance of the same shape (a fact quoted without the axis it varies along): (a) Part B carries a sourced REAL trend (`medicareCostTrend`), so the era's first quotable year is the era's CHEAPEST — "the steady state" was the council's word and it was false; (b) the selection rule "read the first all-enrolled quotable year" was validated on healthnc's two-year gap and has no bound of its own — `COHORT_FADE.full` gates on household EXISTENCE, so a 61/40 household's era year (measured: yearsFromNow 26, cohort 1.00) IS quotable and the rule shouts a ~$8,100 headline the household does not pay for twenty-one years; worse, the enrolled MEDIAN is non-monotonic (up when the younger enrolls, back down when the elder's death crosses the path median), so at that gap the both-enrolled window is a one-year blip (alarming-and-wrong, the cardinal sin's other face). The council's ruled mitigation — "the span bounds the rule" — was wrong at source: the span is the on-ramp's LENGTH and says WHEN; the loud figure is the era year's own total. The rule under a non-monotonic median went to a second council the same night.

## Fix

- **The decision rides the wire.** `HealthYearSink.medicareEnrolled` is pushed beside `medicareBase` from `regime.medicareEnrolledCount` (0 when the year has no regime or nobody is enrolled); `HealthAgg` aggregates it; `buildHealthReadout` emits `HealthReadoutYear.medicareEnrolledP50` (a median of integer counts — an integer except at an exact 50/50 tie). Observe-only: nothing inside the engine reads it, the sink exists only under `healthReadout: true`, so reduce-to-spine byte-identity holds.
- **Both Medicare cards read it.** `medicareEraYear(readout, peopleCount)` = the first quotable year with `medicareBaseP50 > 0 && medicareEnrolledP50 ≥ peopleCount`; the step card's two-of-you fork is `medicareEnrolledP50 ≥ 2`. The age proxy is deleted; `composeHealthSheet`'s `people` type now documents that ages feed ONLY the ACA anchor's biological `count65` for the deduction stack.
- **`medicareAnchor` never moves.** It is consumed once and the step card's MAGI / headroom / two-of-you fork ride it (the two-year look-back hazard lands there); the premium card gained a SECOND year, not a moved first one.
- **The composition has three arms.** DEGENERATE (the era IS the anchor — every all-65+ household — and ONLY that: the council's "or equal rounded totals" collapse was a confirmed blocker in review — one enrollee + a look-back surcharge, 2,700 + 3,100, collides at the $100 grain with two at base, 5,800, and the collapse then spoke the anchor's surcharge under the multi-year frame): the pre-build sentence, byte-identical. ERA: the loud figure is the era year's base + surcharge — never ×2 (5,765 ≠ 2 × 2,703: the base trends across the on-ramp), never averaged, never extras-folded; the on-ramp quoted SECOND with its SPAN ("For about the first two years, while only one of you is on Medicare…"); the surcharge AND the extras each bound PER QUOTED YEAR — by FRAME ("while only one of you is on Medicare" / "once you're both on it") with the figure re-quoted, because two frames can round to one figure; the noun "Part B premiums and any income surcharge" (the wire's surcharge is the combined Part B + Part D IRMAA — the review caught "Part B premiums" alone mis-scoping a surcharged figure while the next line claimed it counted no drug coverage) and the extras' size SPOKEN ("Neither figure counts what you'd pay for drug and supplement plans — … about ~$5,900 a year once you're both on Medicare, and about ~$2,900 a year while only one of you is") because the extras are provenance-mixed (entered / affirmed-zero / a typical placeholder "not an actual bill") and folding a placeholder into a modelled headline is a new calm-but-wrong. NO ERA (never reached inside the quotable window): the on-ramp figure with its frame stated and nothing invented for the years nobody observed. "Start at" carries the trend's direction; the enrollee is named by COUNT ("only one of you") — a median count carries no identity, and a name would re-derive the proxy just deleted.
- **The hero is framed where it lives.** The second council of the night (the era-year rule under the non-monotonic median) refused a persistence floor — a K-gate silences a year the engine priced, the rosy direction — and found that every seat had been editing the SENTENCE while the loud figure two type-steps above it was the unframed channel a reader repeats. The fact's `eyebrow` is the visible caption AND the section's accessible name, so it frames the figure in both channels at zero height: "Medicare premiums while you're both on it" / "…while only one of you is on it"; the era frame became "while you're both on" in all six slots at once (a partial reframe leaves two frame words over one state), and the no-era sentence lost "steady" for the predicate's shared truth, path-scoped. The 61/40 seed (`healthgap`) ships beside it so the residual has an eye to go to.
- **The gate is the real pipeline.** `healthSheetSeedGate.test.ts` runs both shipped Medicare seeds through `buildSpineParams → runEngine → composeHealthSheet` and pins the rendered strings at the $100 grain: `retired` byte-identical to the pre-build card, `healthnc` era-loud with the step card's anchor unmoved. Every fixture dollar in the unit arms is the engine's own number from the same run (DND 012), never the composer's formula. Six planted mutants, each reverted byte-exact: the age proxy restored; the era finder returning the anchor; the wire's count dropped to 0; the wire carrying the biological `count65` (the onset witness — a 66/66 household with one spouse enrolling at sim-year 3 — reds it, and NOTHING else did: the unit's load-bearing split had no witness until the review asked for one); the equal-rounded-totals collapse restored; the extras spoken from one year over both figures.

## Key Insight

When a surface needs a fact the engine DECIDED — who is enrolled, who is priced, who is alive — the fix is a field on the wire carrying the decision, never a re-derivation from the engine's inputs. Inputs are not the decision: the producer's predicate carries guards the mirror cannot see, every guard the producer gains later widens the fork silently, and "single-source it with the other mirror" only makes two consumers wrong together. The tell in code review is a UI comment that says "the same predicate as the engine" — a shared predicate is a fork with a promise attached; a shared FIELD is single-sourced. Three corollaries the same night surfaced: a figure quoted from the FIRST year of a trended era is that era's minimum, so the sentence must carry the direction ("start at"), not a level ("could run"); a collapse keyed on a rounded TOTAL erases the decomposition the sentence then asserts — two frames that round to one figure are still two frames, so bind by frame and collapse only on identity; and a selection rule validated on one household's shape (a two-year gap) inverts on the household nobody tested (a twenty-one-year gap, where the median it reads is non-monotonic) — measure the rule across the shape axis before calling any single quantity its bound. And when a figure misleads, look first at the channel the reader REPEATS — the hero, not the sentence beneath it — and frame it in the element that reaches both the eye and the accessibility tree.
