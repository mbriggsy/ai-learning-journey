# Phase 3 — Controls

**Goal.** Deliver the "pull it toward you" half of the product as a **shippable cold-read milestone before the solver exists**: the itemized **budget builder** (essentials = the survival floor, discretionary = the surplus), the **two manual controls** (withdrawal **sequencing** + **Roth conversion**, both driving the Phase-1 overlays the solver will later search), the **healthcare surfaces** (ACA cliff, IRMAA, HSA) over the Phase-1 healthcare overlay, the **sharpen loop** (every assumption visible and editable, each edit *sharpening* the answer), and **returning-user re-entry** with honest per-surface staleness. After Phase 3 a friend can build a real budget, hand-tune both controls, see the survivor's tax cliff move, and come back tomorrow to a calm, honestly-stale saved answer — all without a recommendation yet.

**Why this is Phase 3.** These surfaces all *consume* the first answer (Phase 2) and the validated spine + overlays (Phase 1) — they make sense only once the confidence statement exists and the engine is deterministic. They are also the entire input + reasoning substrate the Phase-4 solver searches: the budget's two-tier essentials/surplus split *is* the lexicographic objective's two tiers; the two manual controls *are* the solver's search axes; the healthcare overlay is the cliff the solver must not silently optimize over. Building and **cold-reading** them by hand first means the solver in Phase 4 inherits proven, honest control surfaces instead of inventing them — and the manual milestone is itself a real, usable tool. The honesty bar rises here, not later: every control readout that moves real money must wear its hedge.

> Paths are relative to `projects/the-back-nine/`. References: `(R#)` → `docs/brainstorms/the-back-nine-requirements.md` (v2); `(findings §StrandN)` → `docs/research/foundation-findings-2026-06-03.md` (tax = **§Strand 5**, engine/solver validation = **§Strand 4**); `(healthcare doc)` → `docs/research/pre65-healthcare-aca-hsa-2026-06-04.md`. **Reference numbers (brackets, RMD ages, ACA/IRMAA/HSA dollars, applicable-%, Part B premium, validation %) are NEVER inlined here** — every dated tax/health constant lives in the canonical year-keyed `src/engine/constants/` module (cited to §Strand 5 / the healthcare doc, marked **DIRECTIONAL-UNTIL-PINNED**), and this plan points to it.

## Phase-3 cross-cutting contracts

*These eight contracts span the units below. Each is named once here so a reader sees the seams, then enforced in the unit that owns it — mirroring Phases 1 and 2. Several are seams Phase 1/2 explicitly handed forward into a Phase-3 home; this section is that home. The tax and healthcare overlays are **dependencies on Phase 1**, consumed here, not re-specified.*

1. **The tax + healthcare overlays are CONSUMED as Phase-1 dependencies — never re-specified here (the de-duplication).** The Roth/sequencing controls (Unit 10) **drive** the Phase-1 Unit 2 **tax-and-accounts overlay** (per-person pre-tax / Roth / taxable buckets, ordinary-income tax on pre-tax withdrawals + RMDs + the conversion, **Social-Security provisional-income taxation** as a per-year bounded fixed-point, the **MFJ→single** switch at the sampled first death) and the Phase-1 Unit 1 **sequencing substrate** (`sequencing.ts`'s named drawdown policies). The healthcare surfaces (Unit 11) **read** the Phase-1 Unit 3 **healthcare overlay** (the two distinct MAGI calculators — **ACA-MAGI ≠ IRMAA-MAGI** — the pre-65 ACA cost as a per-year fixed-point with explicit 400%-FPL-cliff branching, the IRMAA 2-year-lagged surcharge, the HSA fourth bucket). `roth.ts` here **orchestrates the two arms + the conversion event over those overlays; it does NOT re-implement decumulation, taxes, RMDs, the SS fixed-point, or the ACA fixed-point** — it reuses the same per-year update function so order-of-operations can never drift between the spine and a control. **Reduce-to-spine golden invariant (inherited, re-asserted):** every overlay and every control reduces **byte-identically** to the Trinity/Bengen-validated decumulation spine when off — buckets collapsed to one pool **AND** conversion = 0 **AND** ordinary-tax off **AND** RMD logic inert **AND** healthcare off — the golden cases are never perturbed. All tax/health numbers live in `src/engine/constants/` (§Strand 5 + the healthcare doc), never inlined.

2. **The single-shared-market-draw / CRN rule is structural and now MORE load-bearing (inherited from Phase-1 contract #1; one-line restatement here).** All account buckets — pre-tax / Roth / taxable / **HSA** — share **one** market-return draw per year (the same normals stream as the spine); buckets differ ONLY in tax treatment, never in return assumption. Tax / RMD / conversion / ACA / IRMAA are deterministic post-draw arithmetic consuming **zero** random draws. This lets the two control arms (Unit 10) and the budget's two-track evaluation (Unit 9) read off **identical** futures (signal, not luck), and it **structurally forecloses asset-location** (per-account return assumptions *are* asset-location modeling). Per-bucket draws are forbidden — they triple draws-per-year, change the draw-schedule dimension, and silently break CRN. The draw schedule is a pure function of path/horizon dimensions only.

3. **The death-order conditional-FILTER contract (owned cross-cutting by Units 10 + 12; consumes Phase-1 longevity).** Death-order is an editable hypothetical in **both** the control surface (Unit 10) and the sharpen loop (Unit 12). It is committed to ONE reading: **a CONDITIONAL view that FILTERS the existing path population — never a re-draw, never a re-index of which spouse is the survivor.** Selecting "if one of you outlives the other" conditions on the **sub-population of paths where the chosen spouse dies first** — same seed, same draws, zero re-index — CRN-safe by construction. This is buildable only because Phase-1 longevity **samples per-path, per-spouse death years and retains which spouse died first per path** (the closed-form `P = pₓ + pᵧ − pₓ·pᵧ` mixture alone does not retain survivor identity). **Semantics:** the **default** readout is the engine's full probability-weighted **mixture** ("whoever outlives the other" — the honest number); the conditional view is clearly labelled a hypothetical, **never a prediction** (R12), never a deterministic to-age horizon (§Strand 4 forbids that). **Thin-subpopulation guard:** below a minimum path count the conditional view **widens its hedge / declines a precise frequency** rather than show a noisy number. Death-order, survivor-spending ratio, and per-person retirement years are **single model fields** — an edit from either surface recomputes **both** the spine and any open control under the unchanged seed. The survivor's **MFJ→single threshold flip for BOTH IRMAA and the ACA-FPL basis** rides this same filter (healthcare doc §4e).

4. **The require-the-hedge copyGuard lint (owned by Unit 10; extends Phase-2 Unit 7's copyGuard).** The Phase-2 copyGuard was a ban-list (certainty-hygiene, catastrophe-lexicon, the catalog-enumeration). Unit 10 adds the **new lint shape the reset demands: a positive/require assertion** that **every control readout and every recommendation-class headline CONTAINS a probabilistic hedge** drawn from the typed hedge-token catalog. **It must NOT hand-duplicate the phrase list it checks — it reads the same single-sourced hedge-token set `copy.ts` uses to author the strings** (`burned/063` single-source-the-gate; the roadmap's "the require-the-hedge lint must not hand-duplicate the phrase list it checks"). Built mechanically or hedge-burial drifts in silently (a north-star carried landmine). The surviving ban-list checks (certainty verbs, catastrophe lexicon) stay. **`src/viz` stays string-free** (`TwoFutures` receives all text as props from `copy.ts` at the `src/ui` layer); **all control + healthcare surfaces route through the catalog**, and the slot-discipline allowlist gains the new numeric slots (tax-bracket %, user-echoed conversion amount/years, the survivor tax-cliff `~$X`, the ACA/IRMAA cost `~$X`, the shadow-marginal-rate `~X%`). **The §Strand-3 securities/IA attorney-gate has LAPSED** (personal-tool reset; §Strand 5 "the reg/attorney-gate has LAPSED"): the categorical framing of the Roth surface (contract enforced in Unit 10) is now an **honesty/calm** choice, **not a legal shield** — it is **not** carried as a live gate, and no fixture or copy is blocked on it. *"It's just for friends" never softens this — the hedge is required because the readout moves real money.*

5. **The single schemaVersion-2 ladder (owned cross-cutting by Units 9 + 10 + 11; targets Phase-1 Unit 4's migration).** Three units extend the persisted `model.ts` shape; there is **ONE coordinated bump, not three parallel v2's**: **schemaVersion 2 = account buckets (pre-tax / Roth / taxable / HSA) + per-person birth year + the itemized budget + the sequencing-policy field + the vintage-stamp fields** (tax-table, RMD-age-rule, senior-bonus-sunset, ACA-legislative-status, applicable-%, IRMAA-brackets, Part-B-premium, app-methodology-default — reserved/nullable until populated). Producers/consumers: **Unit 10 writes** the bucket + birth-year + sequencing + tax-vintage stamps; **Unit 11 writes** the HSA bucket + healthcare-vintage stamps; **Unit 9 writes** the budget; **Unit 13 reads** all stamps for staleness. Phase-1 Unit 4's decrypt-branch-on-version migration therefore targets **one coherent v1→v2 shape**. **schemaVersion describes the persisted blob only** (prepended at Unit 4's encrypt step); the in-memory model carries no version. A model-only re-encrypt under the existing in-memory DK (fresh IV, **wraps untouched**) is the gated write path Phase-1 Unit 4 exposes for the v1→v2 upgrade — opening a what-if **never** mutates the vault on disk (the only route to disk is an explicit user Save).

6. **Per-surface staleness mapping (owned by Unit 13).** A staleness clock exists **iff** the engine consumes a corresponding year-stamped parameter, and each clock gates **only** the surface it affects: **wall-time-since-edit + app-methodology-default vintage** gate the **spine verdict**; **tax-table vintage + RMD-age rule + senior-bonus-sunset** gate the **controls/lever only** (the spine is tax-blind — a tax-vintage warning there cries wolf); the **healthcare vintages** (ACA **legislative status**, applicable-% table, IRMAA brackets, Part B premium) gate the **healthcare surfaces**; the **balance-drift confirm** gates **both** (per-bucket for a v2 vault — incl. the HSA bucket); the **budget time-boxed line items whose windows have advanced** (a travel item past its end year; the pre-65 healthcare-gap item once a spouse reaches 65) get a calm **re-confirm**. The tax fixture carries its **OBBBA-2025 legal basis as provenance metadata** (a statutory change reads as a vintage bump, not silent inflation drift). SS provisional-income thresholds are **frozen/not-indexed** (§Strand 5) → **no clock**. **Absent-stamp = not-applicable** (a v1 / spine-only vault is never "infinitely stale").

7. **The controls and the budget are the next R19 surfaces (owned by Units 9 + 10; extends the Phase-1 numeric-domain + Phase-2 Unit 5 semantic-plausibility split).** Unit 10 clamps the conversion to **`[0, remaining pre-tax pool]` accounting for cumulative multi-year conversions**, validates a sequencing policy's per-year legality (RMD met before discretionary ordering; bracket headroom real) **before** mutating plan state (`burned/021` validate-before-mutate), and rejects degenerate amounts. Unit 9 sanity-checks budget line items (non-negative amounts; `endYear ≥ startYear`; a coherent zero-essentials "all discretionary" case warned but allowed). Unit 11 sanity-checks the SLCSP benchmark + HSA balances. All surface calmly inline, **reusing Phase-2 Unit 5's R19 message grammar** — never a silently broken or falsely-confident answer.

8. **The Phase-3 N=1 colorblind cold-read gate + the affordance/copyGuard inheritance (cross-cutting; copyGuard extension owned by Unit 10).** **(a) The cold-read gate** mirrors Phase-2's ceremony but covers Phase 3's emotionally-loaded NEW surfaces, each a Phase-3 **exit condition**: **(i)** the control **two-futures in grayscale** — both arms + the delta legible without hue, the **delta-led reframe** landing as honesty not a bait-and-switch; **(ii)** the **two-tier essentials/lifestyle headline** — "safe / trim travel, not go hungry" landing as relief-with-honesty, never two competing verdicts; **(iii)** the **bad-news sharpen transition** — a worsening verdict lands as honest recalibration, not a gut-punch; **(iv)** the **healthcare cliff / shadow-rate copy** — honest about a >100%-effective-rate conversion, never alarmist; **(v)** the **re-entry staleness + balance-confirm + survivor-recovery copy** — honest, not alarming. The N=1 cold-read (Briggsy) judges **tone**, never correctness (correctness is the automated oracle's job, gating in Phase 4). **(b) Affordance + copyGuard inheritance (plan-level IA decision; pixels deferred to implementation + the cold-read):** all Phase-3 surfaces reuse Phase-2 Unit 7's single **progressive-disclosure affordance** (origin-aware bottom-sheet on mobile, portal to `document.body`), the **shared design tokens**, the Phase-2 Unit 6 **non-color signal grammar** (word + shape + magnitude, never a hue shift — the reader is color blind), and the copyGuard catalog — so the budget builder, the control panel, the healthcare surface, the sharpen entry, the escape hatch, and the staleness notes are consistent **by inheritance**, not re-invented.

- [ ] **Unit 9: Budget builder — itemized, time-boxed, two-tier (essentials = floor / discretionary = surplus)**

**Goal:** Let the user build their real spending as an itemized, categorized, **time-boxed** budget whose structure *is* the lexicographic floor/surplus split — and read the answer as a **two-tier verdict** (essentials safe in X of 10; full lifestyle holds in Y of 10) over the *same* outcome-state set, never a new state.

**Requirements:** R20 (itemized, categorized, time-boxed; essentials = floor, discretionary = surplus); R2 (survival-vs-lifestyle separation, Kitces "trim, not go hungry," no color-alone); R19 (budget sanity); R8 (the budget is the *deepening* of the single-total-spend answer, reached via the sharpen loop, never the on-ramp).

**Dependencies:** P1·U1 (the spine — evaluates both tracks on one draw set), P1·U2/U3 (overlays — the pre-65 healthcare-gap line's cost is income-dependent, computed by U3, not user-typed), P2·U5 (the `memoryModel` orchestrator — a budget edit recomputes through it; R19 grammar), P2·U6 (viz tokens for the two-tier readout), P2·U7 (outcome-state set + copyGuard catalog).

**Files:**
- Create: `src/budget/budgetModel.ts` (the line-item shape + tier + time-box window), `src/budget/budgetToSpending.ts` (compiles the itemized budget → the **two** per-year spending vectors the engine reads), `src/ui/BudgetBuilder.tsx`, `src/ui/BudgetLineItem.tsx`, `src/ui/TwoTierHeadline.tsx`
- Modify: `src/shared/model.ts` (the `budget` field, under the single schemaVersion-2 bump — contract #5), `src/store/memoryModel.ts` (budget edit → recompute), `src/ui/copy.ts` (the two-tier headline + line-item strings)
- Test: `src/budget/__tests__/budgetModel.test.ts`, `budgetToSpending.test.ts`, `src/ui/__tests__/budgetBuilder.test.tsx`

**Approach:**
- **Line-item shape (decided):** `{ category, label, annualAmount, tier: 'essentials' | 'discretionary', window: { startYear, endYear | null } }` — `endYear: null` = lifelong. **Time-boxed** is first-class: a travel item runs years 1–N then stops; the **pre-65 healthcare-gap** item runs retirement→65. Forward-looking **planning** input, not back-looking expense tracking (the Monarch lane is explicitly out — Scope Boundaries). **`startYear`/`endYear` are offsets from each owner's retirement year** (per-person retirement years resolve them to plan-timeline years); a window clamps to `[0, horizon]` and truncates at a per-path death per the survivor-spending composition rule below. **All `annualAmount`s are today's-dollar (real) figures** — `budgetToSpending` emits real per-year vectors and the engine applies the methodology inflation default, matching the spine's inflation-adjusted convention so reduce-to-P2 holds for itemized budgets too; a time-boxed line holds constant in **real** terms across its window unless the discretionary spending-shape multiplier says otherwise.
- **The pre-65 healthcare-gap line is a special, income-dependent slot (the seam to Unit 11):** the budget **reserves** the time-boxed slot (retirement→65 per spouse, Medicare being individual), but its **net cost is computed by the Phase-1 Unit 3 overlay** (ACA premium − PTC, MAGI-dependent), **surfaced through Unit 11** — it is **not** a user-typed flat amount, or the model would silently miss the conversion↔subsidy interaction. The budget line carries a flag "cost computed from healthcare inputs," and U9 leaves the dollar to the overlay. The gap line is **`tier: 'essentials'`** (you cannot go uninsured pre-65 — it is part of the survival floor, counted in Tier-1), one window per spouse ending at that spouse's age 65. Because the dollar is MAGI-dependent and the two tracks spend differently, **it resolves to a *per-track* value** — the essentials-only track's lower spend yields lower MAGI, more PTC, and a **lower net premium** than the full track; U3 computes its dollar inside *each track's own* MAGI fixed point, never one figure shared across tracks.
- **Two-track compilation (the structural realization of the two-tier reading):** `budgetToSpending` emits **two** per-year spending vectors from one budget — an **essentials-only** track (Tier-1 floor) and a **full** track (essentials + discretionary). The engine evaluates **both thresholds against the SAME path population on the SAME single shared draw set** (contract #2): "essentials covered every year" (Tier-1) and "full lifestyle covered every year" (Tier-2). ONE shared draw set (CRN: byte-identical market normals path-for-path) but **two decumulation + overlay passes** — essentials-only and full are distinct portfolio trajectories, never the full-spend run read twice. This is the engine half of R21's lexicographic structure that the Phase-4 solver will later optimize (objective ≡ headline metric); Unit 9 builds the *reading*, Phase 4 builds the *objective over it*.
- **Two-tier headline reading (NOT a 7th state):** the headline composes **two** outcome-state-tagged readings over the *same* engine-owned six-state enum (P2·U7) — an essentials-floor verdict + a lifestyle-surplus verdict ("your essentials are safe in 10 of 10 futures; your full lifestyle holds in 7"). The negative-tier copy is **action-first / Kitces** ("in the other 3 you'd trim discretionary, not go hungry" — R2), and the non-color signal grammar (word + shape + magnitude) carries each tier's state independently. The state-*selection* logic stays engine-owned in `confidence.ts`; `TwoTierHeadline.tsx` is a pure presentation composition of two already-tagged states. **Degenerate collapse:** when the full track is byte-identical to the essentials track (no discretionary present), `TwoTierHeadline` renders the **single-metric** P2 statement verbatim, not two identical tier verdicts — this is precisely what keeps the reduce-to-P2 headline byte-identical.
- **Spending shape is user-set, never solver-recommended:** a go-go-years front-load is a user-set per-year multiplier on the **discretionary** track only (the solver optimizes *funding*, not how you live — north-star carried landmine + Scope Boundaries). U9 owns the shape control and surfaces its honest consequence on the surplus tier; it never proposes a shape.
- **Reduce-to-P2 invariant (the budget's golden anchor):** a budget that is a **single lifelong essentials line equal to the P2 total-spend figure** must compile to the *exact* single per-year spending vector P2 fed the engine → the **saved seed/headline is byte-identical** to the single-total-spend first answer. The single-total-spend answer is the **degenerate budget**; itemization is the deepening, never the on-ramp (R5/R8). Migrating single-total → itemized preserves the seed.
- **Survivor-spending composition (the seam to the survivor floor — R17, the product's emotional core).** The survivor-spending ratio (~75% default, P1·U1) is applied **per-path at the sampled first death** to the compiled tracks: it scales the **discretionary** track and the **discretionary portion of essentials only** — **sticky survival essentials (the survivor's healthcare-gap / single-enrollee Medicare premium, housing, property tax) do NOT scale by the couple ratio**, so the Tier-1 survivor floor is never silently understated. **Degenerate-inert (preserves reduce-to-P2):** in the degenerate single-essentials-line budget (= the P2 total, no discretionary) the composition is inert — the ratio falls back to flat-on-total, matching P2's arithmetic so the survivor-year vectors stay byte-identical. The composition stays out of the P1 spine (it lives in `budgetToSpending`, not `simulate`) and is surfaced as an R7-editable assumption (Unit 12); the simpler ratio-on-total reading is the documented fallback if the cold-read finds the split too fiddly.
- **R19 (contract #7):** non-negative `annualAmount`; `endYear ≥ startYear`; a zero-essentials "everything discretionary" budget is **coherent** (Tier-1 floor trivially safe) but **warned** calmly; clearing all essentials while a required fact is set returns the *floor* tier to a trivially-safe read, not an error. Reuse P2·U5's R19 grammar.

**Patterns to follow:** P2·U7 outcome-state lookup (pure presentation keyed by the engine enum, exhaustive over it — `burned/042` fail-loud-on-contract-drift); P2·U5 `memoryModel` recompute home + R19 grammar; `burned/017` notify-once (write all displayed slices before one notify); colorblind-safe two-track encoding via P2·U6 tokens (separate by luminance, not hue).

**Test scenarios:**
- Happy path: a multi-line budget (lifelong essentials + a 20-yr travel discretionary item) compiles to two per-year vectors; the headline reads two tier-verdicts; the discretionary track drops out after year 20 (time-box honored).
- Edge (reduce-to-P2): a single lifelong essentials line = the P2 total → the compiled vector is **byte-identical** to the single-total-spend answer under the same seed, the survivor-spending composition is **inert** (flat-on-total), and the rendered headline is the **single-metric P2 form**, not a doubled tier pair (the budget's golden anchor).
- Edge (tier-ordering monotonicity — golden invariant): for any budget, Tier-1 (essentials) `X-of-10` ≥ Tier-2 (full) `X-of-10`, asserted **path-for-path** on the shared draw set (every full-success path is an essentials-success path, since net premium is monotone non-decreasing in MAGI); pair the subset-assert with a presence companion that ≥1 path actually stressed the discretionary gap (`burned/027`); a planted budget whose two passes diverge fails loud.
- Edge (survivor floor): on paths past the sampled first death, Tier-1 essentials reflect the ratio applied per the composition rule — sticky essentials (the healthcare-gap / single-enrollee Medicare premium) are **not** scaled by the couple ratio, while discretionary is.
- Edge (real convention): a constant-real multi-year line compiles to the engine's expected **real** per-year vector, not a nominally-flat one.
- Edge (two-tier split over one draw): essentials safe in 10/10 while full lifestyle holds in 7/10 — both read off the **same** path population/draw set (assert identical normals consumed), not two sims.
- Edge (income-dependent healthcare-gap line): the gap line's dollar comes from the overlay (Unit 11), not a typed amount; removing the gap line does not zero a typed value.
- Edge (zero-essentials coherent-but-warned): an all-discretionary budget yields a trivially-safe floor with a calm warning, never an error/indeterminate.
- Error (R19): negative amount and `endYear < startYear` are caught calmly inline (P2·U5 grammar), no falsely-confident answer.
- Integration: editing a budget line in the sharpen loop (Unit 12) recomputes both tiers through `memoryModel` under the unchanged seed; the spending-shape multiplier moves only the surplus tier.

**Execution note:** Test-first on the **reduce-to-P2 invariant**, the **two-tracks-on-one-draw** assertion, and the **Tier-1 ≥ Tier-2 path-for-path ordering** (the budget's golden anchors) before any UI — they prove the budget introduces no new draw, no spurious second verdict, and no survival inversion.

**Verification:** A user builds a real itemized, time-boxed budget; the answer reads as two honest tiers (safe floor + lifestyle surplus) over the same state set; a single-total budget reproduces the Phase-2 answer byte-identically; spending shape is user-set with honest consequences, never recommended. **Passing the N=1 cold-read on the two-tier headline (relief-with-honesty, not two competing verdicts) is a Phase-3 exit condition** (contract #8).

- [ ] **Unit 10: Manual withdrawal-sequencing control + Roth-conversion lever**

**Goal:** Two hand-tunable controls over the Phase-1 overlays — the user picks a **named drawdown policy** (or a custom order) and a **Roth conversion** (amount + years) — rendered as **two futures with the delta as the hero**, headlined by the survivor's tax cliff, as a co-pilot surface that **states every readout with its hedge** and never issues a verdict. Built by **consuming** the Phase-1 tax overlay + sequencing substrate, not re-implementing them.

**Requirements:** R9 (the two coupled controls — manual here, solver-optimized in P4), R10 (the user tunes/overrides; comparative on demand), R11 (calm, invited, never a nagging alert), R12 (probabilistically framed; the hedge required), R4 (detail on demand — the two-futures comparison one tap down), R19 (clamp on the new input surfaces).

**Dependencies:** P1·U1 (CRN seam + `sequencing.ts` named-policy substrate + per-path survivor identity), P1·U2 (the tax-and-accounts overlay — **consumed**, not re-specced), P1·U3 (the healthcare overlay — the controls move ACA-MAGI/IRMAA-MAGI through it), P1·U4 (the model-only re-encrypt write path for the v1→v2 upgrade), P2·U5 (`memoryModel` the lever-open mini-intake extends; R19 grammar), P2·U6 (two-series **encoding tokens** + the CVD/grayscale probe — the de-collision/delta-hero *rendering* is this unit's), P2·U7 (copyGuard + copy catalog).

**Files:**
- Create: `src/ui/SequencingControl.tsx` (named-policy picker + custom-order editor), `src/ui/RothLever.tsx`, `src/ui/RothSurface.tsx` (the quiet categorical teaser), `src/ui/RothTuner.tsx`, `src/ui/RothAccounts.tsx` (lever-open bucket mini-intake), `src/viz/TwoFutures.tsx` (the two-series **component** — built here against `roth.ts`'s real emission, reusing P2·U6 tokens; **string-free**, all text via props), `src/engine/roth.ts` (**orchestrates the two arms + the conversion event over the P1·U2/U3 overlays; does NOT re-implement decumulation/taxes/RMDs/fixed-points**)
- Modify: `src/shared/model.ts` (per-person pre-tax / Roth / taxable bucket fields + **per-person birth year** + the **sequencing-policy** field + bracket-vintage stamp, under the single schemaVersion-2 bump — contract #5), `src/ui/copy.ts` (the control surfaces' strings + the single-sourced **hedge-token catalog**), `src/ui/copyGuard.ts` (the **require-the-hedge** lint + the new Roth/control numeric slots)
- Test: `src/engine/__tests__/roth.test.ts`, `src/ui/__tests__/sequencingControl.test.tsx`, `rothLever.test.tsx`, `src/ui/__tests__/copyGuard.test.ts` (the hedge lint)

**Approach:**

*The two controls drive the consumed overlays (contract #1 — the de-duplication).*
- **The manual sequencing control (net-new):** the user picks a named drawdown **policy** from the decided set **{proportional, taxable-first, pre-tax-first, bracket-fill}** or a **custom per-bucket order**, which `SequencingControl.tsx` passes to `sequencing.ts` (the P1·U1 substrate) to decide which bucket funds each year's net withdrawal — driving the P1·U2 tax overlay (different sequencing → different ordinary income → different SS-taxation, ACA-MAGI, IRMAA-MAGI). The control **picks** a policy; it does **not** rank or recommend one (that is the P4 solver). - Edge (notify-once): a near-margin edit yields either both-stable or both-changed in one frame, never a one-frame mixed pair (`burned/017`).
- Edge (every new assumption present + editable): budget lines, sequencing policy, conversion, tax-payment-source, SLCSP, enhanced toggle, HSA, survivor-SS step-down, survivor-spending ratio, death-order are all reachable and editable (R7 completeness).

- Integration (escape hatch): sets an assumption directly, consistent with the guided path; an escape-hatch edit on an indeterminate answer is defined (non-sticky).
- Integration (shared assumption single-source): editing death-order / survivor-spending recomputes **both** the spine and any open control under the unchanged seed (contract #3).

**Verification:** Every assumption behind the answer — including the budget, both controls, and the healthcare inputs — is reachable and changeable within one interaction; edits move the answer stably (seed-stable, no jitter), narrowing on precision and shifting honestly on a correction; the dollar and headline never flicker against each other. **Passing the N=1 cold-read on the bad-news sharpen transition (honest recalibration, not a gut-punch) is a Phase-3 exit condition.**

**Goal:** A calm re-entry that shows the saved answer (re-derived, not a replayed string) with honest, per-surface staleness awareness across the tax, healthcare, and budget clocks — and routes the surviving spouse back in.

**Requirements:** R4 (the saved answer surface), R8 (re-entry, not re-intake), R15/R16/R17 (unlock + the survivor recovery door + the two-person posture).

**Approach:**
- **Unlock surfaces ALL of P1·U4's outcomes — including the survivor's primary door.** ReEntry is the explicit consumer of P1·U4's four outcomes: **wrong-passphrase** → calm retry, no lockout; **data-damaged** → "use your export" restore; **no-vault / post-eviction** (Safari ~7-day) → restore as a first-class entry; and the **in-place recovery-phrase unlock** — forgotten passphrase + intact vault → unlock via the recovery phrase → set a new passphrase. *The recovery door is the surviving spouse's primary way back in — the flow the product exists to serve.* **Two security obligations (both MUST hold):** **(a)** setting a new passphrase is a **MANDATORY blocking gate** — **no write path is reachable until the new passphrase is set and the `passphraseWrap` re-minted** (fresh salt+IV), or the survivor could save while the on-disk wrap still encodes the forgotten passphrase, silently degrading the vault to recovery-phrase-only access (the worst survivor outcome). **(b)** the recovery-phrase **entry field is masked by default** (reveal-on-hold), and any step that displays the phrase carries the same "store offline, don't screenshot" instruction first-save uses (the phrase is a full credential with a shoulder-surf threat, highest in this high-stress survivor context).
- **Show the saved confidence statement by RE-DERIVING it at unlock** (not replaying a stored string): `memoryModel` recomputes the saved model under its **persisted seed** → byte-identical to the saved displayed value → and this **re-seats Unit 12's session-only sticky baseline** so the first post-unlock edit is sticky against the exact number the user last saw. **Re-PRESENTATION uses the SAVED fixture vintage** (so the number matches what the user screenshotted) with the staleness flag as the honest signal; any **re-RUN** (entering the sharpen loop or re-opening a control) recomputes under the **CURRENT** fixture and explicitly states the assumptions were updated — never silently stale, never silently changed. **(Forward hand to P4·U17:** once a *recommendation* can be saved, a stale saved recommendation is a real **executed action** and its staleness must **invert to re-solve under current fixtures** — "the action we recommended may no longer be advised," not "your number drifted." In Phase 3 only the spine + the manual-control results are saved, so the re-present-under-saved-vintage rule holds — a saved **manual-control** result stays a what-if (re-presented under its saved vintage), and **only a saved *recommendation* inverts** to re-solve-under-current (the boundary the reset turns on: a saved control what-if ≠ a saved executed action); U13 lays the per-surface clock groundwork U17 inherits.)*
- **Per-surface multi-clock staleness (contract #6 — a clock exists iff the engine consumes a year-stamped parameter, and gates only its surface):**
  - **wall-time since last edit** + the **app methodology-default vintage** (new conservative-return/longevity defaults shipped since save AND never user-overridden → a calm "we've updated our default assumptions" note; user-overridden assumptions are immune) → gate the **spine verdict**.
  - **tax-table vintage** (the §Strand-5 bracket/standard-deduction fixture, carrying its **OBBBA-2025 legal-basis as provenance** so a statutory change reads as a vintage bump, not inflation drift) + the **RMD-age rule** (birth-year-derived; the legislated 73→75 step in 2033) + the **senior-bonus-deduction sunset** (a calm note when a pre-2029 answer is viewed in/after 2029) → gate the **controls/lever only** (the spine is tax-blind). *(SS provisional-income thresholds are frozen/not-indexed — §Strand 5 — so they have **no clock**.)* A saved control result is flagged stale when **any** of {bracket vintage, RMD-age rule, senior-bonus-deduction sunset} has moved (matching the three control-gating tax clocks this bullet enumerates — not a narrower pair); when stale and the user **opens** the control, the surface shows the vintage at the control ("computed with [year] tax brackets") and does not present the survivor-cliff figure as current — `staleness.ts` exposes a queryable "tax-table stale" flag.
  - **Healthcare vintages (net-new) → gate the healthcare surfaces:** the **ACA legislative status** (the load-bearing clock — cliff-on vs enhanced; the re-verify-at-build status), the **applicable-% table**, the **IRMAA brackets**, the **Part B premium**. A saved healthcare cost is flagged stale when any has moved; the ACA-status clock is the most consequential (it can flip the whole pre-65 calculus). The status note is dated and honest.
  - The **undetectable balance-drift clock** (real portfolio drifted; no aggregation): a calm **"is this still your balance?"** confirm before presenting the saved verdict as current. **For a v2 vault it confirms the per-bucket split** (pre-tax / Roth / taxable / **HSA**), not just the total — the pre-tax pool's drift relative to Roth changes the control calculus more than the total does; a changed split routes into the **Unit-10 mini-intake**, a changed total into the **Unit-12 sharpen loop**. SS staleness is folded in as "are these still your benefit amounts?" (SS benefits are user-entered, not a year-stamped fixture — no separate clock).
  - **Budget time-boxed clocks (net-new) → gate the budget surface:** a budget line whose **window has advanced** since save (a travel item now past its `endYear`; the pre-65 healthcare-gap item once a spouse has reached 65) gets a calm **re-confirm** ("your travel budget was set to end last year — is that still right?") rather than silently spending past its window.
- **Absent-stamp default:** a v1 / spine-only vault (saved before the stamps existed, or by a user who never opened a control) has no tax/health stamp → `staleness.ts` treats an **absent** stamp as **"not applicable"** (no false stale), never "infinitely stale." Stamps live on the model so staleness is computable offline.
