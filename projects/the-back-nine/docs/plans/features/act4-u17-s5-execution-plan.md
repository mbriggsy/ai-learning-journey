## The three rulings S5 was built to (Briggsy, 2026-07-26)

1. **The no-vault tap routes into the ceremony.** The gesture mints onto the draft and opens firstSave,
   mirroring `deriveResultSave`'s `unsaved → {kind:'first'}`. The control is never dead on the
   no-vault path — that path is every dev seed, the Caddie walk and the fit gate. **The CTA names the
   escalation BEFORE the tap.**
2. **The CTA tells the whole-plan truth up front.** `session.save()` writes the ENTIRE scenario
   (`session.ts:596`), so the tap also commits unsaved edits. The control says so before the tap — not the
   confirmation after. A CTA implying a note-only write is calm-but-wrong.
3. **The record card is MINIMAL** — that a saved read exists, its age, whether it still holds, and the
   re-open CTA naming its cost. It does **not** quote the remembered grade/verdict enums. Those stay
   persisted, so the card can grow later with no schema change. *(Ruling 3 has a live amendment
   pending Briggsy's own call: the S6 cold read hard-flagged the shipped card for naming no strategy at
   all, which sharpens the case for putting the saved action in frame — an amendment to his own written
   ruling, recorded at [`docs/caddie/cold-read-log.md`](../../caddie/cold-read-log.md) and tracked in
   [`docs/backlog.md`](../../backlog.md) as "The saved-record card does not name the strategy". Nothing
   about the minimal card was reversed; that half is still open. The card's OTHER S6 flag — the
   superseded verb — was fixed and shipped 2026-08-03 (`bd851f24`): `recommendRecordSuperseded` now
   reads "It may no longer fit the two of you.", a claim about the ADVICE, and copy.ts carries the
   standing prohibition on ever making it a showing-verb again.)*
