## Why (one paragraph)

An all-65+ household was priced **$0 Medicare every year** — base Part B (the figure lives once, in `partB2026.standardPremiumMonthly`, `src/engine/constants/health.ts`) and the IRMAA surcharge both — because `taxOverlay.ts:1545` gates all Medicare pricing on `acaTable !== undefined` (= `healthcareEnabled`), and intake set that flag only via `healthcareOn`, which requires the ACA quote pair AND a pre-65 member (`healthcareOn` at `intakeMap.ts:580`, over `healthcarePriced` at `intakeMap.ts:733-740`). Gross SS enters cash at full value (`simulate.ts:305,376`), so net cash was overstated by the full premium: the cardinal calm-but-wrong-OPTIMISTIC sin, live on the core already-retired demographic — the exact subject of the U11 honesty-hawk veto. The machinery to fix it **already existed and was tested** (`medicareAnnualCost` healthOverlay.ts:689-700; the 2-yr lagged feed-forward taxOverlay.ts:1545-1583; CMS-pinned constants health.ts:145-177; the seed the household already answers at intake, spread onto params unconditionally at intakeMap.ts:660). The unit opened one gate, honestly, on both routes.

## The red team's verified mechanism fact (the unit was built against it, chair- and pilot-verified at source)

`dateSearch.ts:229` forces `healthcareEnabled: true` on **every date candidate** (docstring :144-145). So a **still-working all-65+ household already priced Medicare on the date route** before this unit — while the age-keyed `medicareUnpriced` predicate told them "Medicare's own costs … aren't priced into these numbers … would pull the picture down some, never up," a **live false statement** in the pessimistic double-discount direction (insight 080). That predicate and its copy key `verdictMedicareUnpriced` were both deleted here; what shipped in their place is the pricing-fact predicate `showMedicarePricedNote` (`healthSheetChrome.ts:336`) and the paired copy `verdictMedicarePriced` (`copy.ts:1048`) rendering with `verdictMedicareResidual` (`copy.ts:1060`). The spine $0 gap was the optimistic sin. The unit fixed both; each is named with its true direction in any narration.

## HARD CONSTRAINTS (hawk-armed conditional vetoes — all four honored in the shipped build)

1. **The disclosure predicate keys off the run's ACTUAL route-aware pricing decision, NEVER off ages** (and off no age proxy like "all-65+ AND seedComplete"). `showMedicarePricedNote` takes no `people[]` parameter at all, so the age-mutation witness is type-level: there is no age left to mutate.
2. **The affirmative "Medicare is priced in" line ships TOGETHER with the narrowed residual disclosure** — the affirmative alone (implying ALL Medicare is priced) would be a new optimistic lie.
3. **The real-flat Part B disclosure shipped** (Tier-1 mandatory — the one residual leaning optimistic). *Superseded 2026-07-19:* the Medicare-cost-trend sourcing unit prices Part B **trended**, and the residual's still-flat clause narrowed by swap to the extra-coverage premiums alone ([architecture.md §7.2](../../architecture.md)).
4. **The copy reconciliation (spendHelp) was NOT deferred** — it rode this commit.
