# Phase 3 — Depth on Demand

**Goal.** Deliver the "pull it toward you" half of the master principle: the sharpen loop (every assumption visible and editable), the one differentiated Roth-conversion lever, and the returning-user re-entry with honest staleness.

**Why this is Phase 3.** These surfaces all *consume* the first answer — they only make sense once the confidence statement exists (Phase 2) and the engine is deterministic (Phase 1). The Roth lever is the differentiation success criterion and the regulatory tripwire, so it lands after the spine is trustworthy. Re-entry closes the loop for returning users.

**What the deepening changed (2026-06-04).** The pre-deepening plan framed `roth.ts` as a thin "CRN consumer" of the Phase-1 engine. It is not: the Phase-1 engine is **tax-blind** (one pooled portfolio, `withdrawal == spending`, no accounts/taxes/RMDs/brackets/filing-status), so the survivor "tax cliff" — the lever's entire headline — exists nowhere in it, and a tax-blind delta is **sign-inverted** (it sees only the cash drain of paying conversion tax, so every conversion looks worse). The lever genuinely needs a **tax-and-accounts overlay** on the engine, structured exactly like the earned-income bridge (a CRN-safe per-year cash-term transform that reduces byte-identically to the validated spine when taxes are off). **Briggsy's call (2026-06-04): model the most complete honest picture — Social-Security taxation IN, IRMAA/ACA/NIIT/state OUT-but-disclosed.** This re-opens deepened Phase 1 as a tracked amendment (the same way the bridge did); the full cascade is the ledger at the end of this file.

## Phase-3 cross-cutting contracts

*These eight contracts span the units below. They are named once here so a reader sees the seams, then enforced in the unit that owns each — mirroring Phases 1 (4 contracts) and 2 (7 contracts). Several are seams Phase 1/2 explicitly handed forward into a Phase-3 home; this section is that home.*

1. **The tax-and-accounts overlay (owned by Unit 8; cascades to Phase-1 Unit 1 as a tracked amendment).** The Roth lever cannot be computed against the tax-blind spine engine. Unit 8 adds a **multi-account + federal-tax overlay** to `simulate.ts`: per-person **pre-tax / Roth / taxable** buckets, ordinary-income tax on pre-tax withdrawals + RMDs + the conversion, **Social-Security provisional-income taxation**, and the **MFJ→single** bracket switch at the sampled first death. **It is structured exactly like the earned-income bridge (Phase-1 contract #1 / Phase-2 cascade #7a): a deterministic per-year transform of the cash-flow term, indexed by absolute year, consuming ZERO random draws — CRN-safe by the identical argument.** Sign nuance to encode precisely: the bridge **nets down** the withdrawal (income reduces it); the tax overlay **grosses up** the cash needed / applies a tax outflow (tax increases it, and the conversion adds a tax bill paid from the taxable bucket) — same per-year-cash-term shape, opposite sign. **Gated OFF by default:** the invariant's conditions are **exhaustive** — buckets collapsed to one pool **AND** conversion = 0 **AND** ordinary-tax off **AND RMD logic inert** (no forced distributions: "taxes off" alone does **not** silence RMDs, which are a forced-distribution mechanic, not a tax — an active RMD on a single collapsed pool would perturb the per-year order-of-operations the spine never performs). Under all four, `netWithdrawal == spending` and the engine is **byte-identical to the Trinity/Bengen-validated decumulation** — the golden cases are never perturbed (the Roth analogue of the bridge's `earnedIncome=0` invariant). The overlay is ON only for the Roth arms. **SS provisional-income taxation is a per-year bounded fixed-point** (taxable-SS → provisional income → tax → gross-up → re-converge; deterministic, zero draws — CRN-safe; the convergence rule is pinned in the engine seam; numbers + the not-inflation-indexed landmine in §Strand 5). `roth.ts` orchestrates the two arms and the conversion event; it does **not** re-implement decumulation — it reuses the same per-year update function so order-of-operations can never drift between the spine and the lever. **MVP tax scope is pinned in findings §Strand 5** (IN: brackets, standard deduction + age-65 + senior-bonus, RMDs, SS-taxation; OUT-but-disclosed: IRMAA/ACA/NIIT/state). All tax numbers live in **§Strand 5**, never inlined here.

2. **The single shared market draw per year (owned by Unit 8; one-line note to Phase-1 contract #1).** Splitting the portfolio into three buckets tempts an implementer to shock each bucket with its own return draw — which would triple draws-per-year, change the draw-schedule **dimension**, and silently break CRN. It would **also** breach the regulatory line: per-account return assumptions *are* asset-location modeling ("hold growth assets in the Roth"), the #1 IAA tripwire (findings §Strand 3). **One rule fixes both: all buckets share ONE market-return draw per year (the same normals stream as the spine); buckets differ ONLY in tax treatment, never in return assumption.** Tax/RMD/conversion are deterministic post-draw arithmetic. This makes "no asset location" a *structural* guarantee, not a copy promise.

3. **The death-order CRN-safe conditioning contract (owned cross-cutting by Units 7+8; cascades to Phase-1 contract #1).** Death-order is an editable hypothetical in **both** the sharpen loop (Unit 7, R7) and the Roth lever (Unit 8). It is a **new CRN-shifting input** Phase-1 contract #1 never enumerated (it enumerated only the survivor two-regime boundary + the earned-income bridge). **The mechanism is committed to ONE reading (the deepening review caught a drift between two): death-order is a CONDITIONAL view that FILTERS the existing path population, never a re-draw or a "swap which spouse is the survivor" re-index.** The engine samples both spouses' deaths per path from fixed per-spouse longevity lanes; selecting "if [one of you] goes first" conditions on the **sub-population of paths where the chosen spouse dies first** — same seed, same draws, zero re-index — so it is CRN-safe by construction. **Semantics (decided):** the **default** survivor readout is the engine's full probability-weighted **mixture** over both orders ("whoever outlives the other" — the honest number); the conditional view is clearly labelled a hypothetical, **never a prediction** (R12) and never a deterministic to-age horizon (findings §Strand 4 forbids that). **Phase-1 requirement (tracked cascade — feasibility/adversarial review):** the conditional view is buildable ONLY if Phase-1 longevity **samples per-path, per-spouse death years and retains which spouse died first per path** (or exposes per-path death-order); the closed-form `P = pₓ+pᵧ−pₓ·pᵧ` mixture alone does **not** retain survivor identity, so this must be a hard Phase-1 longevity requirement, not assumed. **Thin-subpopulation guard:** for a large age/sex longevity gap the opposite-order sub-population can be a small fraction of paths, making a conditional "X of 10" rest on a few paths — below a minimum path count the conditional view widens its hedge / declines a precise frequency rather than show a noisy number. death-order, survivor-spending ratio, and per-person retirement years are **single model fields** (Phase-2 contract #1e); an edit from either surface recomputes **both** the spine and any open lever under the unchanged seed.

4. **The stateful sticky-rounding seating (owned by Unit 7; the seam Phase-1 #1 + Phase-2 #1d handed forward).** Phase 2 built **no** stickiness (intake numbers move freely). Unit 7 seats it for edits of an **already-complete** answer, and must honor three sub-rules the seam already defines: **(a) two distinct margins** — `lastDisplayedHeadline` **and** `lastState` (copy-state / verb-tense) gate on the **band-edge** margin; `lastDisplayedDollar` gates on the **independent distance-to-next-display-rounding-step** margin. Conflating them onto one margin is the exact "one sticky while the other flickers, since they render in one sentence" failure Phase-1 #1 warned about. **(b) notify-once write ordering** (`burned/017`): a single recompute writes **all** displayed-value slices + the `lastDisplayed*` stickiness **before** the one notify/render — never a partial commit that paints a sticky number next to a flickering one. **(c) lifecycle:** the sticky baseline is **captured when the answer first becomes a resolved-verdict** (engine state ≠ indeterminate; it stays free-moving while indeterminate); `lastDisplayed*` are **session-only, NOT persisted to the vault**; on re-entry they are **re-seated from the deterministic recompute of the saved model** (Unit 9), so the first post-unlock edit is sticky against the exact number the user last saw — and a genuinely-moved answer (staleness) is never held to a stale screenshot value.

5. **The single Phase-3 schemaVersion ladder (owned cross-cutting by Units 8+9; cascades to Phase-1 Unit 2).** Both Unit 8 (account buckets) and Unit 9 (tax-table + vintage stamps) extend the persisted `model.ts` shape. **There is ONE coordinated bump, not two parallel v2's:** **schemaVersion 2 = account buckets + the vintage-stamp fields (reserved/nullable until populated).** The tax-table-vintage stamp is **written by Unit 8** (the producer — `roth.ts` records which §Strand-5 fixture vintage it computed against) and **read by Unit 9** (the consumer, for staleness). Phase-1 Unit 2's decrypt-branch-on-version migration therefore targets one coherent v1→v2 shape. **schemaVersion describes the persisted blob only** (it is prepended at Unit 2's encrypt step) — the in-memory model carries no version, so "v1 vs v2" is an on-disk distinction.

6. **Per-surface staleness mapping (owned by Unit 9).** A staleness clock exists **iff** the engine consumes a corresponding year-stamped parameter, and each clock gates **only** the surface it actually affects: **tax-table / RMD-age / senior-bonus-sunset** vintage gates the **Roth lever only** (the spine is tax-blind — a tax-vintage warning on the spine verdict would cry wolf); the tax fixture carries its **OBBBA-2025 legal basis as provenance metadata** (so a statutory bracket change reads as a vintage bump, not silent inflation drift — it is fixture provenance, **not** a separate runtime clock); **wall-time + app-methodology-default vintage** gate the **spine verdict**; the **undetectable balance-drift** confirm gates **both** (and, for a v2 vault, confirms the **per-bucket split**, not just the total). The vestigial "SS COLA/wage-base" clock from the pre-deepening plan is **dropped** (SS benefits are user-entered, not a year-stamped engine fixture) and replaced by an "are these still your benefit amounts?" confirm folded into the balance-drift clock.

7. **The lever is the third R19 surface (owned by Unit 8; extends Phase-1 contract #4).** Phase-1 contract #4 split R19 into the engine's numeric-domain half + Phase-2 intake's semantic-plausibility half. The Roth lever adds **new input surfaces** (`RothAccounts` bucket mini-intake + `RothTuner` amount/years) that fall under neither today. The conversion must be **clamped to `[0, remaining pre-tax pool]` accounting for cumulative multi-year conversions**, degenerate amounts rejected, surfaced calmly inline reusing Unit 3's R19 message grammar.

8. **The Phase-3 N=1 colorblind cold-read gate + the copyGuard / affordance inheritance (owned cross-cutting; the copyGuard extension owned by Unit 8, cascades to Phase-2 Unit 5).** **(a) The cold-read gate** mirrors Phase-2 Unit 5's ceremony but covers Phase 3's emotionally-loaded NEW surfaces (Phase-2's gate predates them). **Named first-class subjects, each a Phase-3 exit condition:** **(i)** the Roth two-futures in grayscale — both arms + the delta legible without hue, and the **delta-led reframe** landing as honesty, not a bait-and-switch; **(ii)** the **bad-news sharpen transition** — a worsening verdict lands as honest recalibration, not a gut-punch; **(iii)** the **re-entry staleness + balance-confirm + survivor-recovery copy** — honest, not alarming. **(b) copyGuard** — Phase-2-load-bearing **by construction** (catalog-enumeration + inline-string ban, scoped to `src/ui` + `src/intake`) — must reach the Roth strings: **`src/viz` stays string-free** (`TwoFutures` receives all text as props from `copy.ts` at the `src/ui` layer), **all four Roth surfaces** (categorical teaser, tuner readout, "buys you ~N years" headline, death-order frame) route through the catalog, and the **slot-discipline allowlist gains** the Roth numerics (tax-bracket %, the user-echoed conversion amount/years, the survivor tax-cliff `~$X`). **(c) Affordance inheritance (plan-level IA decision; concrete pixels deferred):** all Phase-3 surfaces reuse Phase-2 Unit 5's single **progressive-disclosure affordance** (origin-aware bottom-sheet on mobile, portal to `document.body`), the **shared design tokens**, and the **non-color signal grammar** — so the sharpen-loop entry, the escape hatch, the death-order control, the staleness notes, and the `RothAccounts` mini-intake are consistent **by inheritance**, not re-invented. Concrete affordances, curves, empty/loading/error states, and exact copy are deferred to implementation + the N=1 cold-read (the plan-level decision is the inheritance, not the pixels).

- [ ] **Unit 7: Sharpen loop + assumption editing (R7/R8) + escape hatch**

**Goal:** Make every assumption visible and editable, so each added precision visibly (and stably) *sharpens* the answer — and a power user can set any assumption directly.

**Requirements:** R6 (escape hatch), R7 (every assumption visible+editable), R8 (refinement *sharpens* the answer — see the edit-class split below).

**Dependencies:** Units 1, **3** (it **extends** Unit 3's `memoryModel` — the recompute home + the single plaintext shape — so Unit 3 is a dependency, matching Unit 8's correct citation), 5.

**Files:**
- Create: `src/ui/SharpenLoop.tsx`, `src/ui/AssumptionPanel.tsx`, `src/ui/EscapeHatch.tsx`
- Modify: `src/store/memoryModel.ts` (edit → recompute; seat the sticky-rounding fields — see Approach)
- Test: `src/ui/__tests__/sharpen.test.tsx`, `assumptions.test.tsx`

**Approach:**
- **Every assumption is listed and editable, across TWO classes (widens the pre-deepening "on-the-user's-behalf" scope).** (a) **on-behalf methodology assumptions** — returns, inflation, longevity, **survivor-SS step-down, survivor-spending ratio, death-order hypothetical**, **and the Roth tax-payment-source assumption (Unit 8)** — these survivor + tax assumptions are R7 obligations the engine makes invisibly today; (b) **required user-facts** — ages, savings, spending, incomes, per-person retirement years. The second class is required because **Unit 9 routes a confirmed-changed balance into this sharpen loop** (savings is an *asked* fact, not an on-behalf assumption), so Unit 7 must edit required facts too, not only methodology defaults. Editing a required fact can legitimately return the answer to **indeterminate** (e.g., clearing savings) — the sharpen surface handles the indeterminate state by reusing Unit 5's indeterminate copy.
- **Each edit re-runs the engine through the Phase-2 `memoryModel` orchestrator** (it does **not** re-own the recompute home — it **extends** it) with the **same seed** → the answer **morphs** (Unit 4's draw-once-then-morph, never a re-draw) and the cross-edit margin-gated crossfade Unit 5 defined is **exercised here** (Phase 2 tested it only with synthetic prop recomputes).
- **R8, honestly: "sharpens," not unconditionally "tightens" (the band-narrowing claim is mechanically true for only ONE edit class).** Distinguish: **(a) CONFIRM/NARROW** an epistemic default (replace a wide conservative assumption with a more confident one) → the band genuinely **narrows** — this is R8's "rewarding" case. **(b) OVERRIDE a value** (enter real higher spending, a longer longevity table, a higher return σ) → the band **shifts and may widen**, and the verdict can worsen — framed calmly as "a truer picture," never a failure. **"Monotonic, no jitter" is the SEED-stability guarantee** (same seed → no sampling noise), **NOT** a promise every edit narrows. *(R8's origin wording "visibly tightens" → tracked requirements amendment to "sharpens: narrows on added precision, shifts honestly on a corrected value" — same posture as the existing six-state / ~8-input amendments. Cascades to Unit 4's morph contract, which must support widen/shift, not only narrow.)*
- **This unit seats the stateful sticky-rounding (Phase-3 contract #4).** It adds `lastDisplayedHeadline` / `lastDisplayedDollar` / `lastState` to `memoryModel` and the sticky rule, applied to edits of an **already-complete** answer, keeping the X-of-N headline, the **dollar figure**, and the **copy-state / verb tense** stable under tiny changes (Phase-1 contract #1, Phase-2-amended). **The two margins are wired separately** (headline + `lastState` ← band-edge margin; dollar ← independent display-rounding-step margin), all slices are written **before one notify** (`burned/017`), the baseline is **captured at the resolve→verdict transition**, and the fields are **session-only / not persisted** (re-seated on re-entry from Unit 9's deterministic recompute).
- **Honest about the epistemic vs aleatory floor.** Sharpening narrows input uncertainty but cannot narrow market randomness — copy for "you've hit the floor, more precision won't narrow it further," and for "sharpening revealed bad news" (entering real, higher spending makes the verdict worse — handled calmly; R8's "rewarding" cannot promise only-good-news). The worsening-verdict transition carries the **same non-color signal grammar** as Unit 5's state→state crossfade — word + shape + dollar magnitude, **never a hue shift** ("bad news" is never expressed by color alone; the N=1 reader is color blind) — and is one of the Phase-3 cold-read subjects (contract #8). **Floor detection (decided, MVP-minimal — keeps Phase 1 closed):** a **Unit-7-local heuristic** — when an edit collapses an input's uncertainty toward a point and the resulting band-width change is below a defined threshold (the band is already near its market-randomness-only width), surface the floor copy. *(The alternative — a `confidence.ts` "band-width attributable to inputs vs returns" decomposition emission — would re-open Phase 1; deliberately NOT chosen for the MVP. If the local heuristic proves too coarse in the N=1 cold-read, escalate to the engine emission as a tracked Phase-1 amendment.)*
- **Escape hatch:** jump straight to any assumption without walking the guided path (R6). The hatch can target an assumption while the answer is still **indeterminate** (a required fact is still unknown) — the sticky seam applies only to a complete answer, so an escape-hatch edit on an indeterminate answer has defined behavior (no stickiness, Unit 5's indeterminate render).

**Patterns to follow:** `useCallback` slider/panel handlers (`ai-journey-stats/007`); portal panels (`burned/013`); **`burned/017` (notify-once: write all store slices before notify)**; **`burned/005` (generation counters for stale slider/cooldown timers and `onAnimationComplete` — see Unit 8's *Live tune + stale-callback discipline*)**; **`burned/015` (put press/`:active` feedback on a CHILD of any motion-animated control — the library's inline transform beats the CSS cascade)**.

**Test scenarios:**
- Happy path (narrow case): confirm a wide conservative default with a more confident value → band **narrows**; same seed → no jitter.
- Edge case (override case): enter higher real spending → band **shifts/may widen** and the verdict can worsen → calm "a truer picture / sharpening revealed…" copy, not a cheerful tightening (proves the two edit classes are distinguished).
- Edge case (aleatory floor): an edit whose band-width change is below the floor threshold → the floor copy appears (heuristic fires); a normal edit does not falsely trip it.
- Edge case (sticky two-margin split): an edit that crosses the **dollar** rounding step **without** crossing a **band edge** updates only the dollar figure (headline + verb-tense hold); the mirror case holds the dollar while the headline crosses — proving the margins are wired separately, not collapsed.
- Edge case (notify-once): a near-margin edit yields **either both-stable or both-changed in one frame**, never a one-frame mixed pair (`burned/017`).
- Edge case (every survivor-phase assumption present + editable): survivor-SS step-down, survivor-spending ratio, death-order are all reachable and editable (R7 completeness check).
- Edge case (required-fact edit): clearing savings returns the answer to **indeterminate** with Unit 5's indeterminate copy (proves Unit 7 edits required facts, not only on-behalf assumptions).
- Integration (escape hatch): sets an assumption directly and the answer updates consistently with the guided path; an escape-hatch edit on an indeterminate answer has defined (non-sticky) behavior.
- Integration (shared assumption single-source): editing death-order / survivor-spending here recomputes **both** the spine and any open Roth lever under the unchanged seed (Phase-3 contract #3).

**Verification:** Every assumption behind the answer is reachable and changeable within one interaction (trust success criterion); edits move the answer stably (seed-stable, no jitter), narrowing on added precision and shifting honestly on a corrected value; the dollar and headline never flicker against each other.

**Approach:**

*The tax-and-accounts overlay (Phase-3 contract #1 — the substance of the lever).*
- The spine engine is tax-blind; the lever needs ordinary-income tax on pre-tax withdrawals + RMDs + the conversion, tax-free Roth growth, **Social-Security provisional-income taxation** (Briggsy's call, 2026-06-04 — the most complete honest picture; SS-tax is core to the survivor's real bracket, so it is *in* the lever, not the "full-return modeling" R9 defers), and the **MFJ→single** filing switch at the sampled first death. **All of it is a deterministic per-year transform of the cash-flow term, indexed by absolute year, consuming ZERO random draws** — CRN-safe by the same argument the earned-income bridge earned (it grosses the withdrawal **up**, where the bridge nets it **down**). **Both arms run at identical tax fidelity** — the with-arm and the without-arm both decumulate through the overlay; the sole difference between arms is the conversion event. **Golden invariant (required test):** with the buckets collapsed to one pool, conversion = 0, and taxes off, the Roth "without" arm is **byte-identical (same seed) to the spine's single-portfolio distribution** — the Roth analogue of the bridge's `earnedIncome=0` invariant; this anchors the delta as honest signal and prevents engine drift between the two code paths.

It is surfaced as an **R7-editable assumption** (it lives in Unit 7's assumption panel) and **disclosed next to the delta**, with an **honest degradation note when no taxable bucket was entered** (taxable is optional). This is distinct from — and does not violate — the "don't model a tax-payment-source *optimization*" rule (§Strand 3): a calculator must *pick* a source to compute at all; it just must not *optimize* it.
- The **MFJ→single boundary is the joint→survivor two-regime boundary Phase 1 already models** (no new boundary; the empty-nest retired couple gets **no QSS grace** — files single the year after the first death; §Strand 5).

*Quiet categorical surface (R11, regulatory — Phase-3 contract #8).*

This is the categorical→personalized boundary (findings §Strand 3 — the "ballgame").

A structural assist already exists (account buckets aren't collected until lever-open, so the pre-tax pool **cannot** drive the trigger) — but income/MFJ status is known at surface-time, so the categorical-trigger rule is stated explicitly and **tested** (the visibility predicate reads no personalized financial field). **IRMAA/ACA framing stays OUT** even though §Strand 3 lists IRMAA as legally-safe tax-talk — legally-safe ≠ in-MVP-scope (R9). The exact teaser copy + the trigger condition + the no-asset-location boundary are **flagged for the §Strand 3 securities/IA attorney-gate** before going load-bearing.

*Account-type buckets, collected on lever-open (the Phase-2→3 handoff), in TWO cases.*
